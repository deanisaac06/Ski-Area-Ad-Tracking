Afternoon And Twilight Tickets
Afternoon Tickets at Schweitzer are available Sunday - Thursday so that you can hit the slopes any time after 12:30pm and ride until the lifts close! Afternoon Tickets are not available on days with Twilight Skiing.
Blackout dates: 12/26 - 12/31, 2/15, and 1/18/2026
Twilight Tickets are available on Fridays, Saturdays, and select holidays. You can purchase tickets in advance for the best price and to support the local nonprofit organization, Own the Night! Own the Night tickets must be purchased at least one day before your visit for the best price and to support our local non-profits. Twilight Tickets are available for purchase on the day of your visit online or at the ticket window for full price.
Please note: While Twilight Tickets are unavailable for online purchase on MLK Weekend and Presidents Weekend. Twilight Tickets for these holiday weekends must be purchased in-person at the ticket window in the Village.
