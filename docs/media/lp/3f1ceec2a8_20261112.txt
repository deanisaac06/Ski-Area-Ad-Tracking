Why Rent with Jackson Hole?
Whether you’re renting for a day or all season long, Jackson Hole’s got you covered. Rent everything you need, including skis, snowboards, snowshoes, apparel like ski jackets and ski pants, and equipment like ski poles and snowshoes. To streamline the pick-up process, guests can purchase equipment online and pick it up anytime after 3 pm the day before. Alternatively, utilize our ski rental delivery service, JH Ski Delivery, for ski and snowboard rental delivery to your doorstep.
Enjoy the flexibility to drop off or exchange rental equipment at any of our rental locations!
Get a 15% ski rental discount when you book online today.*

What is the Gold Standard?
Across Jackson Hole, our rental experts share one mission: to make your days on snow effortless and fun. Teton Village Sports, JH Sports, Hoback Sports and JH Ski Delivery together are united under Gold Standard Rentals, the Jackson Hole Mountain Resort-owned and operated rental network designed to give you the best and most flexible rental experience in the valley.
Look for the Gold Standard Rentals badge to unlock benefits like:
The largest selection of top ski & snowboard brands in Jackson Hole
Exchange or return your gear at any JHMR-owned shop  in the Village or in town
Overnight gear storage at any base-area location
Complimentary performance tune added to your rental package
Save time: Pick up your lift tickets with your rentals
