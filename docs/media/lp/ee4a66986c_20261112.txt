KIDS 6-12 SKI FOR FREE WITH PURCHASE OF 2+ NIGHTS OF LODGING
LODGING TICKETS ARE CURRENTLY UNAVAILABLE ONLINE
Make your family ski getaway even better with up to two FREE Junior Lift Tickets for each night of your stay, per reservation, when paired with paid Adult Lodging Add-On Lift Tickets. 
How it works: 
For each night of lodging booked, your reservation is eligible for up to two free Junior Lift Tickets (ages 6–12). Free junior tickets are based on the number of nights booked—not the number of ski days during your stay. Tickets may only be picked up at the Lodging Front Desk.
