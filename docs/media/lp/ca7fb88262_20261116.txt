Value Card
Even More Savings This Season – $30 off Nearly every Lift Ticket!
The Value Card is your ticket to mountain savings all season long and we have increased the discount to $30 off lift tickets!
Save every time you ski or ride – use your Value Card to save $30 on All-Day, 4-Hour, and Twilight lift tickets.
1 FREE Lift Ticket valid for use one day in March 2027 included with purchases through February 28, 2027.
Only 5 blackout dates for All Day Tickets: January 16–17, 2027 and February 13–15, 2027.
Never blacked out for Twilight tickets – ski/ride the night away all season long!
Reload your Value Card with lift tickets at jiminypeak.com to go direct to the lift when you arrive at the resort.
Purchases made between November 16, 2026 and February 28, 2027 will receive a free lift ticket valid one day during the month of March 2027.
Buy Now
Rules & Information:
Jiminy Value Card discounts may not be combined with any other discounts.
Multi Day tickets are discounted and do not qualify for an additional Value Card discount.
Jiminy Value Cards do not come preloaded with lift tickets. Lift tickets must be purchased and loaded on the card after initial purchase.
Lift tickets will be available to load anytime after November 1st.
When purchasing a Jiminy Value Card please choose the card based on the appropriate lift ticket for your age. Choose either Adult (19-64) or All Other Ages (<19/>64). Value Cards may only be re-loaded with like tickets (ex. Adult lift ticket on Adult Savings Card).
The Jiminy Value Card is non-transferable and for use only by the owner of the card. It may only be used once per person, per card, per day.
Free ticket must be redeemed at the Ticket Window, with the Jiminy Value Card present.
Purchase of the Jiminy Value Card after February 28, 2027, will no longer include a free ticket.
There is a $5 replacement fee for a lost Jiminy Savings Card.
