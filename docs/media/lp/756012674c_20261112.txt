Breakfast at Home and Walk to the Lifts. It’s that Easy.
Stay in Teton Village
Staying in Teton Village makes a family ski vacation seamless. Choose from the many family friendly accommodations right in the village and you're never more than a short walk from the lifts.
Many lodging options come with a full kitchen, so you can make a big breakfast, pack lunches for the mountain, and get everyone fed without hunting for a table. Then just step outside and stroll to the lifts.
No shuttles, no parking lots, no hauling skis across a lot with tired kids. At the end of the day, the walk back is just as easy. Kick off the boots and cook dinner on your own schedule, or head out for a bite nearby when no one feels like cooking.
STAY IN TETON VILLAGE
Kitchen Included
Big ski days start in the kitchen. With a full kitchen, you can get everyone fueled up before first chair and pack lunches and snacks for the hill.
Jackson Hole Resort Lodging has plenty of houses and condos in Teton Village that work great for families, with room for the whole crew to spread out.
When the lifts close, cook a big family dinner and swap stories from the day, or bundle up and stroll to one of the restaurants in Teton Village. No car to load, no driving in the dark, and no designated driver needed.
One Resort, Two Mountains. Terrain for Everyone. 
Après Vous Mountain is Made for Families
Jackson Hole is actually two mountains, Rendezvous and Après Vous, and Après Vous is where families spend their time. It’s our beginner and intermediate mountain, built for first days on skis, laps with the family, and gaining confidence on intermediate terrain.
The Teewinot lift, which is accessed directly from the base, is as mellow and flat as any beginner slope in the country. It’s the perfect place for a child or beginner to take their first ever ski turns. Each run ends at the base, giving you plenty of opportunities to check in, regroup as a family, or take a break for a warm lunch.
When your crew is ready for a challenge, Rendezvous Mountain is right there, home to the legendary steeps we’re known for. It’s perfect for the more advanced skiers in the family, or anyone who progresses quickly and wants to push themselves.
