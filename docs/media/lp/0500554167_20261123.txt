welcome to
grand targhee
The story of Grand Targhee isn’t merely the story of a mountain resort. It’s the story of something even bigger, something that lives within the people who love this place, as well as within the place itself. It’s the story of a feeling, and of how that feeling changes people. Sometimes for a day, sometimes for a week. And sometimes for life.
It's also the story of a resort that is blessed with over 500" of Teton powder annually, and where nearly 100 miles of hiking and biking trails bring summer to life. One where honoring and celebrating the culture of people who play in the mountains is always the priority.
Confluence is the first-ever opportunity to own property at Grand Targhee and in the midst of this community.
