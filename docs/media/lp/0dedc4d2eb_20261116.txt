Helpful Tips
Made for Snow Day Memories
Winter at Massanutten transforms the mountain into a snowy wonderland, with 23 ski and snowboard runs, Virginia’s best snow tubing hill, ice skating, and year-round Indoor Waterpark fun. From first turns and fireside après-ski meals to Saturday comedy nights and off-slope adventures, every snow day becomes part of an unforgettable seasonal escape for all ages.
