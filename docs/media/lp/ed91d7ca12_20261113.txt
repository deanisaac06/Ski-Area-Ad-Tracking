WHAT TYPE OF TICKETS DOES THE 50% OFF DISCOUNT APPLY TO?
WOW Card holders are eligible for 50% off regularly priced, full day / full mountain tickets. Your 50% ticket discount cannot be combined with any other discount (online, advance purchase discounts, for example).
In the event of reduced lift ticket pricing, card holders will enjoy 50% off regularly priced, full day / full mountain lift tickets.
