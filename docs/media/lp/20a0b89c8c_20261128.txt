TREMBLANT’S 24H
From December 11 to 13, 2026, the mountain and the village will come to life with the 24h Tremblant, all in support of the great cause for children! Over 24 hours, over 4 500 participants will take turns walking, running, skiing, or alpine hiking. All united by the same goal: to raise funds for children well-being and health.

KIDS SKI FREE WEEK
Tremblant is proud to participate in the Kids Ski Free week organized by Alterra Mountain Company. From December 5 to 11, 2026, kids 12 and under can enjoy full days of skiing or snowboarding, free of charge!
For the occasion, we are offering 50% off equipment rentals and group lessons (from December 7 to 11). It's the perfect opportunity to share your love for the mountain!
