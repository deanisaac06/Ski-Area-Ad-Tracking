PARTNER PASSHOLDERS: If you are visiting Monarch with a season pass from one of our partner resorts, please stop at our ticket window with your valid partner pass, confirmation that the pass is for this season and an ID for a Monarch ticket RFID card (not available online). There will be an initial $5 fee for the card. All of your Monarch eligible days will be loaded on the card, so you’ll only have to do this once. The card is then reloadable with future visits (available online using the RELOAD option). Don’t lose your card! The $5 fee applies to each new or replacement card.
