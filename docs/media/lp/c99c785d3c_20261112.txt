Which pass is right for you?
Visiting once or twice: A single-day lift ticket gets you on the mountain without any commitment. Choose from full day, afternoon, twilight, or night depending on when you want to ride.
Here for a few visits this season: The S-Card is the smarter move. The upfront fee includes your first day at a lower rate, and every visit after that is discounted too.
Planning to ski all season: A season pass gives you the best value and the most flexibility. Head up any day, all season long, with perks available for every visit. Explore all of our season pass options.
Still not sure? Call our ticket office at 1-801-223-4849 and we'll help you find the right fit.
