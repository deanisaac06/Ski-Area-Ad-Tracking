DIRECT-TO-LIFT MULTI-DAY TICKET
Looking to bypass the ticket office this season? This direct-to-lift multi-day ticket allows you to store your preferred payment directly to a reloadable RFID ticket and bypass the ticket office all season. For each day you head to Santa Fe Ski Basin and scan at the gate, your preferred payment type will automatically be charged the appropriate daily rate. No activation fee, no visiting the ticket office, no waiting in line for a ticket on a powder day. Store using our guest app, launch our app on your phone, or visit an office. We are committed to providing a seamless experience.
You will be charged a one time $5 convenience fee for the RFID card.
No Refunds on tickets or convenience fees.
You are responsible for any charges associated to your RFID card while at any ski resort or Ski Santa Fe operated facility.
This ticket is non-transferable.
