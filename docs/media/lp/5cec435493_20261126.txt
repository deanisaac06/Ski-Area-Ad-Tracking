AMBER’S UPDATES: SOLITUDE OPENS NOVEMBER 19
Link some turns on Thursday Friends, the wait is over. On Thursday, November 19, Link chairlift will start spinning at 9:00 a.m., and Solitude will officially kick off its 69th winter season. I’ve been counting down to this one, and I know a lot of you have too. This fall we called it Hike Park, but for a lot of locals it was really Hope Park. Seeing so many of you out there enjoying pre-season park features on Easy Street kept the stoke alive for our whole team while we worked toward Opening Day. Thank you for showing up, for your patience, and for reminding us why we do this. Easy Street will continue to host park features as we get our larger parks up and running. Built by Wintermakers None of this happens without our team. Our snowmaking and mountain operations crews have brought incredible grit, creativity, and around-the-clock effort to get us here, and this year they have some new tools to work with. We’ve made another multimillion-dollar investment in snowmaking, adding new fixed and mobile snow guns, including three new fixed guns on Main Street, plus system-wide improvements that increase coverage and capacity across the mountain. It builds on the more than $5 million we invested in snowmaking last season, all in support of our commitment to Utah’s longest ski and ride season. When you see a snowmaker this week, give them a high five. They’ve earned it. What to Expect on Opening Day Link opens at 9:00 a.m. with access to Easy Street, and Moonbeam Express will follow close behind. Lifts run from 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., and we’ll keep opening more terrain and lifts as Mother Nature joins the team and delivers the goods. A few things to know before you head up: No parking reservations needed. Parking is free with no reservation through the early season. Reservations begin December 18 for Fridays – Sundays and holidays before 11:00 a.m. Free parking every day after 11:00 a.m. New this season, this one came straight from your feedback. Carpools of four or more always park free, and Solitude and Ikon Pass holders ride the UTA Ski Bus for free. Buy lift tickets online and in advance for the best price. The ticket windows at Moonbeam open at 8:00 a.m. if you need your pass or ticket printed. Unlimited Ikon Pass access. Solitude is Utah’s only unlimited Ikon Pass destination, so there’s no need to save your days. Good to go — food, drinks, and gear. Moonbeam Lodge, Argenta Pub, and Stone Haus will be open, along with our rental and tune shops and Canyon Fever. Pay attention to traction law. Big Cottonwood Canyon requires 4×4/AWD vehicles with proper snow tires while the traction law is in effect. Check UDOT Cottonwoods before you head up. DJ + Donuts this weekend Can’t make it Thursday? Join us Saturday for our DJ + Donuts Opening Party at the Moonbeam base area, with free donuts starting at 8:00 a.m. and a DJ keeping the party going until 4:00 p.m. (valid lift ticket or season pass required). Solidaze Returns! Solidaze, our popular pass holder rewards program is back and even better than before. Every day you ski and ride counts towards rewards and prizes.
