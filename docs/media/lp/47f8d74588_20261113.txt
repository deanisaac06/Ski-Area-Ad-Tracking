Why buy a Season Pass?
A Season Pass is an incredible value. You’ll get unrestricted access to the Nordic Center trail system and all the lift-accessed downhill skiing and snowboarding thrills you can squeeze into your 2026-2027 winter schedule with no stops at the ticket windows necessary!
The savings come quickly! Ski or ride as little as 7 days, and you’ll already be saving. That's less than twice per month over a winter season. After that, it's like skiing for FREE! The more days you ski, the more you save!!!
At 49° North you get MORE! Our expanded 7 day a week operations give you more options and more flexibility to enjoy the slopes on your schedule. The only high-speed lift in Eastern Washington means less time in lift lines and just a 7-minute ride to the summit, giving you more time actually to ski and snowboard! You’ll get more season thanks to the region's most extensive snowmaking system, providing earlier opening days and longer seasons. And you’ll find more trails and terrain to explore at Eastern Washington's largest ski area, 49° North!
Follow your true North! Whether your passion is chasing first tracks, arcing turns on pristine groomers dancing through the trees in deep pow, or sending it in the park, 49° North has a little something to feed everyone’s soul. Whatever your true north is, you’ll be able to follow it at 49° North Mountain Resort
Get even more with a 49° North & Silver Mountain Combo Pass!   When one ski area isn’t enough to feed that hunger, it’s time to step up to the best value in the region and get yourself a Combo Pass! You’ll get unrestricted access to both 49° North and Silver Mountain, plus all the perks that come with each, for one low price! Combined, you’ll get 3,925 acres, 14 lifts, 170 trails, 4 summits, and 4,051' of vertical to ski or ride all season!
