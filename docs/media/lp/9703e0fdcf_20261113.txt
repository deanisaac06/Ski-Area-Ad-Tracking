Why become
les Sommets member?
More skiing, less waiting. Become a Les Sommets member. Reload your GoPasse online or pick it up after your first purchase to head straight to the lifts.
Enjoy access to 5 resorts and the longest ski season in Québec, with a 0% payment plan. Plus, get exclusive discounts on lift tickets for you and your guests, savings on tune-ups, in-store purchases, and private lessons. And with the Ski Guarantee, there’s no risk: if unused before December 15, 2026, get a credit, a refund, or roll your pass over to the next season.
More benefits, more skiing, more freedom.
Get started now!

Nearly $30 Million Invested to Transform
the les Sommets Experience
Because the guest experience is at the heart of everything we do, les Sommets is investing nearly $30 million in new attractions, modernized facilities and meaningful improvements across its recreational experiences in the Laurentians and Outaouais. These investments are designed to create even more ways to enjoy the mountain, summer and winter, while enhancing the four-season experience today and for years to come.
