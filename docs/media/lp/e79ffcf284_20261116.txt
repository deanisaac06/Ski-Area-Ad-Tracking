WHAT'S IN YOUR 6IXPAK?!
Mount Washington 6IXPAKs are a group of six deeply discounted activity tickets that can be used any day of the winter season. Available for both Alpine and Nordic access.
Split your 6IXPAK to share with friends, give to family, or use entirely for yourself. Either way, you'll be enjoying savings, convenience, and flexibility for your winter mountain adventures.
NEW this season: Every Alpine 6IXPAK ticket is good day and night! + 2 Bonus night ski tickets in every pack!
LIMITED SUPPLY Alpine and Nordic 6IXPAKs are available online. Alpine 6IXPAKs are also sold at your local Quality Foods starting October 15th, and Nordic 6IXPAKs at the Raven Lodge. Wondering how it stacks up? See single-ticket prices or jump to the comparison.
