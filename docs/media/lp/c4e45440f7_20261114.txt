Warren Miller's Days Off Admission
Kick off ski season with a night of big-mountain inspiration! The Warren Miller film showing of "Days Off" is on Saturday, November 14 at 7:00 PM in the Acropolis Ballroom at Greek Peak.
Berger's Ski and Board Shop is the sponsor and will be there with special displays and giveaways. There will also be a cash bar and exciting information about Greek Peak's upcoming 2026-27 season.
Doors open at 6p.  Movie starts at 7.  Tickets are limited and we expect this event to sell out quickly, so get your tickets today!
To purchase tickets, please select November 14 on the calendar below.
