5:30 AM update – Good morning, skiers and riders! Welcome to the Eldora snow report for Wednesday, November 25, 2026.
Eldora is officially open for alpine skiing and riding! We couldn’t be more excited to welcome you, our devoted community members, back for another great season at your backyard mountain. We are so proud of our incredibly hardworking employees, whose relentless efforts have allowed us to open this week. They deserve a HUGE round of applause.
Terrain: We'll have 1,000 vertical feet of downhill riding and skiing on Hornblower and International trails, from the summit of Challenge Mountain to the base area, served by Alpenglow chairlift. No beginner terrain is available—nor is uphill access, Nordic skiing, or snowshoeing—but we're pushing to open those zones of operation as soon as conditions permit. A small Woodward Terrain Park will be available near West Wing Lodge. Please note that valid passes or tickets are required to access the hike park and the mountain in general. For more on what to expect today, read our Opening Day blog post here.
NWS Weather Snapshot: Sunny, with a high near 39. West wind 11 to 16 mph, with gusts as high as 28 mph.
Safety: No trees or glades will be open. Expect thin, early-season conditions on Opening Day and beyond. Be prepared to ski and ride safely and responsibly on Opening Day and all season long, bearing in mind Your Responsibility Code every time you hit the slopes.
Uphill/Nordic/Snowshoe Access is currently CLOSED. For the cross-country skiers, snowshoers, and uphill skiers out there, Eldora’s Nordic trail network and uphill access routes are NOT YET OPEN. Stay tuned here for updates.
Parking: We will begin charging fees for single-occupancy vehicle (SOV) arrivals sometime in December. Stay tuned here for updates on when fees will begin. Learn more about parking at Eldora here.
Stay informed: Before heading to the mountain this winter, always check this page, eldora.com/conditions, for the latest information and operational updates. You can also sign up for text alerts so you never miss a beat. Text one of the following keywords to (866) 965-6224 for alerts (message and data rates may apply).
ELDORA for general alerts
UPHILL for uphill-specific updates
NORDIC for Nordic updates.
Thanks for checking the Eldora snow report. We'll see you on the hill!

GENERAL INFORMATION
Lift Hours: 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM on weekdays, 8:30 AM to 4:00 PM on weekends. Lifts currently open: Alpenglow only.
Lodge Hours: 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM on weekdays, 7:30 AM to 4:30 PM on weekends. Lodges currently open: Timbers, West Wing, Lower Indian Peaks Lodge.
Pass & Ticket Services, Rental, and Retail: Please visit Lower Indian Peaks Lodge. 
Single-Occupancy Vehicle (SOV) Fees: Vehicles with two or more occupants may park for free seven days a week. Vehicles carrying only a driver will be charged $10 on weekends, during certain holiday periods, and on weekdays with 10 or more inches of new snow. Credit/debit is accepted; cash is not.
