A Different Way to Winter
With 140 kilometers of groomed Nordic trails for skate skiing, classic skiing, snowshoeing, and winter fitness, Royal Gorge offers endless ways to explore the forests, the wide-open expanse of Van Norden Meadow, and the panoramic ridgelines of Donner Summit in the Sierra Nevada near Lake Tahoe—all under your own power.
Some guests come to challenge themselves with one of winter's best full-body workouts. Others come to slow down, explore new trails, and reconnect with nature. From first-time skiers discovering the joy of Nordic skiing to lifelong athletes chasing their next adventure, everyone arrives with a different goal—but they all leave inspired to go beyond.
As part of the Sugar Bowl family, Royal Gorge invites you to experience a different side of winter on Donner Summit, where adventure, exploration, and movement come together to create one unforgettable mountain experience.
