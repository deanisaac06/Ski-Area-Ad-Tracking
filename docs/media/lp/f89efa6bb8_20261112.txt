Dining at Sundance Resort
More than a meal. It’s cuisine with character.
Here, food is art. It’s a from-scratch experience that tastes different, not just because of the mountain-air setting but because such thoughtful care went into sourcing every ingredient and crafting every menu item, every time. Whether you’re savoring a special evening at the Tree Room or splitting an order of nachos on the mountaintop’s Bearclaw Cabin, expect every bite and every sip to be an expression of this place. While we’re honored by the accolades our restaurants have received, what we care about most is serving you an experience you couldn’t have anywhere else.
