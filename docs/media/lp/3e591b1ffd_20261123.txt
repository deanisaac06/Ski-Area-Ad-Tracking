Ski in Jeans Day
Friday, December 4th
It's back! Get your denim on and join us for the 4th annual Ski in Jeans Day - look forward to major discounts on lodging, lift tickets, drinks and more!
This unique event invites skiers and snowboarders of all levels to hit the slopes in their finest denim. Participants can enjoy discounted lift tickets and rentals while rocking their favorite pair of jeans on one of the wildest mountains in the West. After an exhilarating day on the snow, join us in the village for après-ski celebrations featuring a mechanical bull, special giveaways, drink deals, line dancing, free live music, and more. To keep the fun going, discounted weekend lift tickets will also be available for Friday through Sunday.

Ski In Style
Be apart of tradition! 
Friday, Dec. 4
For the past four years, we’ve kicked off the season with a nod to our Western roots and a whole lot of fun. In 2024, we broke the world record for the most skiers and riders dressed in denim. Now, we’re carrying on the tradition, celebrating our Western heritage, bringing a little style to the slopes, and kicking off the season in true Jackson fashion.
