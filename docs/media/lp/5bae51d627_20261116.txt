FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS:
GIFT CARDS
Q1: WHAT CAN SNOW BUCKS GIFT CARDS BE USED FOR?
A: Snow Bucks can be used for anything at the resort: Food, Drinks, Lift Tickets, Tubing Tickets, Equipment Rentals, Lessons, and Ski Shop like Gear, Apparel, and Accessory purchases.
Q2: DO SNOW BUCKS GIFT CARDS EXPIRE?
A: No, Snow Bucks gift cards never expire. They retain their full value until used.
Q3: CAN I USE A GIFT CARD TO BUY LIFT TICKETS OR TUBING TICKETS ONLINE?
A: Yes, but please keep in mind purchasing a gift card does not guarantee Ticket availability. Many Tickets sell-out in advance during Peak Days. If giving as a gift, consider booking the Ticket for a specific date, along with the gift card.
Q4: WHERE CAN I PURCHASE SNOW BUCKS GIFT CARDS?
A: Snow Bucks gift cards can be purchased anytime online through our website or in-person at Guest Services and the Ski Shop during operating hours.
Q5: CAN I CHECK MY GIFT CARD BALANCE?
A: Yes, visit the Gift Card Balance page on our website or ask at Guest Services to check your remaining balance.
