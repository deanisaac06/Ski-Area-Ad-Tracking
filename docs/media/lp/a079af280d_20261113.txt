Your key to the mountain
Lift tickets are valid any day of the 2026/27 season. No blackouts, no restrictions. With the freedom and flexibility of an any-day ticket, you decide when the mountain calls.
Multi-day lift tickets must be used on consecutive days. 
For ages five and under, the mountain is always free. 
For questions, please contact the ticket office at 802-824-5522 ext. 710 or see us in person.

Military Discount
Save $15 off the window rate of 1-day tickets with valid Military ID.
Discount is only valid for 1-day lift tickets and must be purchased with proof of military service:
U.S. Armed Forces personnel on active duty or active reserve status.
Dependents of U.S. Armed Forces personnel on active duty or active reserve status. (Discount good for an individual dependent only)
Military Retirees (individual only, no dependents) 
Adaptive Snowsports Athletes
Adaptive snowsports athletes are eligible for $15 off the 1-day lift ticket window rate. Not valid on half-day or multi-day tickets, and cannot be combined with other offers.
Buddy Ticket Discounts
All Bromley season passholders receive 5 buddy tickets per season. Bring your buddy to our ticket window to redeem $15 off the 1-day ticket window rate!
