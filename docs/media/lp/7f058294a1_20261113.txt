Basin Flex Card
Your Days, Your Call
You know how many days you want on the mountain this winter. You just don’t know which ones yet. With the Basin Flex Card, you don’t have to. Grab 2 to 5 days now, then use them on fresh snow days, bluebird afternoons, or the weekend friends come to town. Any day of the 2026-27 season, no blackout dates.

How Basin Flex Works
Choose your days. Pick 2, 3, 4, or 5 days for adults, young adults, or youth.
Ski any day. No reservations, no set dates, and no blackout dates. Weekdays, weekends, and holidays are all open, so you can ski whenever it works for you.
Grab your card and go. Pick up your Flex Card on your first visit, or head straight to the lift if you already have one. From there, your card takes care of the rest. Each day you ski automatically counts as one Flex day.
Keep the season going. Want more days? Add them to your card anytime.

Where to Pick Up Your Flex Card
On your first visit, scan the QR code on your confirmation email at one of the self-service kiosks in the base area to grab your Flex Card. You can also print your card at the Meadow Pavilion, or if you’re renting gear, pick up your Flex Card and equipment in one stop at the Grizzly Rental Shop inside the Grizzly Center.
Already have a card? If you chose to load your Flex days onto your existing card, there’s nothing to pick up. Head directly to the lift on your first day.
Questions About Basin Flex?
Toggle accordion
Do I need to reserve my days?
No. Just show up on any day you want to ski and go directly to the lift once you have your card.
I already have a Snowbasin card. Do I need to pick anything up?
No. If you chose to load your Flex days onto your existing card, you can head directly to the lift on your first day.
Can I buy more days later?
Yes. Basin Flex is available all season. When you buy more days, you can load them onto your existing card to skip the $3 new card fee.
Can I have more than one Flex Card product?
Yes. If you buy more than one, all of your days live on the same card and stay active until they’re used.
Can I give Basin Flex as a gift?
Yes. Basin Flex Cards make a great gift, but they’re valid for one person and cannot be transferred throughout the season. The same individual must use all days.
Do my days need to be consecutive?
No. Use your days on any dates you choose during the 2026-27 season.
When does it expire?
Basin Flex Cards expire April 30, 2027.
Are there blackout dates?
None. Your days are valid any day of the season.
Basin Flex Cards are non-transferable. Misuse of this card or its privileges will result in immediate and permanent loss of the card and all privileges, and up to a $1,000 fine.
