KidsRule Ski & Snowboard Lessons
KidsRule is a fresh, dynamic program designed to help kids ages 4–14 learn and progress in a safe, supportive, and fun environment. Our group lessons build skills, confidence, and a love for the mountain and sliding on snow.
Full- and half-day lessons are offered Friday–Sunday and daily during holiday and vacation periods. Camps include lift access, and lunch is provided for full-day lessons.
Advanced reservations are highly recommended. For our youngest adventurers (ages 3–5), 60-minute one-on-one lessons are also available.

Why Learning at Cranmore is Different
We believe learning to ski or snowboard should be fun. We use Terrain Based Learning™, a groundbreaking method using custom-built terrain to teach skiing and snowboarding in a comfortable, pressure-free environment. Groups of penguin campers (ages 4–6) keep an instructor-to-camper ratio of 1-5, while adventure campers (ages 7–14) keep a 1-8 ratio.
Beginner Basin, our intro terrain, is designed for gradual skill development while reducing the fear and stress of skiing around more advanced skiers and riders. Beginner Basin features include:

Organized and Easy
Pre-arrival coordination and our upgraded Arlberg Children's Center makes everything easy—from registration to equipment rental. Parents can easily find where their child needs to go.
Our Instructors
Our fun-first learning methods are nurtured by the dedication of our SnowSports instructors. For them, skiing is more than just a fun winter activity, their goal is to share the passions and joy we experience on the hill.
Whether your child is new to snow sports or looking to get back on the slopes, KidsRule has a group and instructor ready to make their day awesome.
What is your child's ability?
Children are assigned to groups based on their ages and abilities. Please use the Snowsports Level Chart shown to ensure your child is in the correct ability group.
