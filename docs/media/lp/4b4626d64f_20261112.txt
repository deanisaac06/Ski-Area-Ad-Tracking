RESTAURANTS, FOOD TRUCKS & MARKET
RESTAURANTS, FOOD TRUCKS & MARKET
From hearty breakfasts to get you going for a day in our champagne powder to cozy fireside evening dining, Big White has plenty of places to eat for breakfast, lunch, après and dinner. There are many mouthwatering options with a price range to suit every budget!
Some restaurants offer:
Black Diamond Bar & Grill
The Black Diamond Bar & Grill is a contemporary Canadian steak and seafood restaurant. Take in the breathtaking Monashee mountain views from the restaurant while enjoying lunch, après or dinner.
VISTA at Black Forest Day Lodge 
Discover Big White's most breathtaking dining destination. VISTA at Black Forest Day Lodge transforms every meal into a memorable mountain experience, offering the perfect blend of comfort food and stunning scenery that makes this sunny bistro an essential stop on any ski adventure.
Clocktower Coffee Co.
Experience Big White's ultimate ski-in, ski-out coffee destination. Clocktower Coffee Co. has become the beating heart of the Village Centre Mall, serving exceptional handcrafted drinks, fresh-baked treats, and grab-and-go favorites.
Cabin Kitchen + Bar
A place where warm hospitality meets elevated comfort food. Inspired by the flavours of Canada, from Okanagan apples to maple syrup and wild game, the menu is a curated journey through the country’s culinary tapestry.
Happy Valley Day Lodge
Discover Big White's premier family dining destination, perfectly positioned steps from the famous skating rink and Happy Valley Adventure Area. With four distinct dining concepts under one roof, Happy Valley Day Lodge has the perfect mealtime experience sure to satisfy everyone in the family. From quick grab-and-go options, unique burger experiences, table service après dining, to a contemporary fine dining steakhouse perfect for date night, Happy Valley Day Lodge has it all!
Moonlight Bistro
Escape to Big White's outpost retreat, where rustic charm meets comfort. Located at the base of Gem Lake chair, Moonlight Bistro embodies a cabin-in-the-woods atmosphere while delivering warming beverages and quick serve meals and treats that are well earned.
Kettle Valley Steakhouse
Located on the top floor of the Happy Valley Day Lodge, our beloved award-winning dining destination returns this Winter with bold flavours, warm hospitality, and a wine list deeply rooted in the Okanagan.
Fat Marmot
Located at the Inn at Big White — the perfect place to warm up, wind down, and dig in after a day on the mountain. One location, two restaurants.
The Woods
Discover Big White's upscale casual dining destination, where chalet heritage meets elevated classics. Located in the heart of the Village with stunning views of Easy Street ski run, The Woods combines a central location with walking distance convenience and great hospitality, food, craft cocktails, and Okanagan wines in a relaxed atmosphere.
Snowshoe Sam's
Perched at the top of the Village, Snowshoe Sam’s is the longest operating pub on the mountain, has been voted the #1 Ski Bar in North America, and is not to be missed! Locals and holiday-goers alike flock to Snowshoe Sam’s for its welcoming atmosphere, warm hospitality, and fun times. The menu combines (somewhat) traditional pub fare with more eclectic dishes, all sure to fill your belly after a day on the slopes!
Moose Lounge
The revitalized Moose Lounge has reopened in Happy Valley Day Lodge, bringing back some favourite traditions.
SPUDS at The Ridge Day Lodge
Experience Big White's most playful dining concept, where passion for the potato reaches new culinary heights. SPUDS at The Ridge Day Lodge combines quick-serve convenience with creative comfort food, delivering customizable dishes revolving around everything that is potato.
