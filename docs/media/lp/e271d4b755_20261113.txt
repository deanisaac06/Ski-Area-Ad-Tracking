PASS BENEFITS
Epic Mountain Rewards: Included with every pass, Epic Mountain Rewards gets you 20% off food, lodging, equipment rentals, group lessons, Epic Mountain Express, and even heli-skiing!
Discount Tickets for Friends: Want to ski & ride with friends and family? Help them get on the mountain and save up to 50% off the window rate of Lift Tickets with Epic Friend Tickets.
Epic Coverage: If something unexpected happens, we've got you covered. Included with every pass at no additional charge, Epic Coverage provides refunds for personal injury, job loss, or resort closures.
Summer Benefits: Before the winter season, enjoy free summer scenic lift rides and 20% off bike rentals, golf, food, and lodging at Participating Resorts.
ADDITIONAL DISCOUNTED TICKETS
Half-price Pass Holder tickets available during peak restricted dates at Heavenly, Northstar, and Kirkwood.
Half-price Pass Holder tickets available during peak restricted dates at Breckenridge, Park City, and Keystone, also at Crested Butte, Stowe, Mount Sunapee, Okemo, Stevens Pass, and beyond 5 days.
Advanced Lift Ticket Rate available during peak restricted dates and beyond 5 days at Vail and Beaver Creek.
