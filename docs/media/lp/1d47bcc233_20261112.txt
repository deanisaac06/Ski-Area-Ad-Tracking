MOUNTAIN SPORTS SCHOOL
SKI & SNOWBOARD LESSONS
We want you to have your best day on the slopes of Alyeska, and our dedicated professional staff is here to help make sure of it. Everyone from the first-time skier or snowboarder to mountain enthusiasts looking to improve their personal skiing or riding technique in carving, powder, steeps, or terrain parks can benefit.
Alyeska’s Mountain Sports School has a level of instruction catered to every skier and rider’s ability. Our programs are structured to teach participants how to improve their technique, read and adjust to changing snow conditions, expand terrain selection, and increase mountain awareness, knowledge, and safety.
Most of all, our Mountain Sports School is here to maximize your smile while on the slopes. Not sure what runs you should be tackling? Find out here.
Questions? Contact us at learn@alyeskaresort.com or 907-754-2280.
Please note, private lessons starting from the hotel must be able to ride the lift. We do offer an online only price discount for most lessons. You must book prior to your arrival to take advantage of this special discount.

PRIVATE LESSONS
Maximize your ski or snowboard experience with instruction tailored to your personal goals and ability level. Everyone from first-timers to experts will find themselves progressing on the mountain after a private lesson with our professional instructors.
Choose a Half Day or a Full Day lesson that can accommodate up to a group of three at an additional cost. Lessons from the hotel must be able to ride the lift. If you wish to meet your instructor at the Hotel, you can request to do so in the comments section of the checkout process.
The purchase of a private lesson allows access to the Mountain Sports School line at each chairlift, whenever accompanied by your instructor. This exclusive line will get you fast tracked to the front of the lift line which maximizes your time on snow with your lesson. This is a great option for a family that wants to stay together for the day, or for a group of kids that feel comfortable skiing together and are of similar ability levels.
