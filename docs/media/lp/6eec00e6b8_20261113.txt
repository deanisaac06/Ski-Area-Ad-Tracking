Reload Flex Passes
If you originally purchased a lift ticket or flex pass online and still have the flex pass/lift ticket card, you may reload it online for a discounted rate. Once you reload your flex pass, make sure to bring it with you to the resort. If we have to reissue you another flex pass card, there will be a $5 new media fee.

THE FINE PRINT: REFUNDS & OTHER DETAILS
Flex passes are non-transferable (i.e. they are only valid for the guest assigned at the time of purchase), non-refundable, and are only valid during the current ski season (unused days will not roll over to future seasons). Flex passes are revocable for misconduct. No refunds will be issued for unused days due to weather or road conditions/closures.
