FALL ACTIVITIES AT BLUE MOUNTAIN
Fall Foliage, Adventures, and Events
Whether you’re here for breathtaking fall foliage in the Pocono Mountains or outdoor adventure, Blue Mountain has something for everyone. Take in the views on a scenic lift ride, zip and climb through the treetops, tackle the ropes courses, or join us for festive Halloween events throughout the season. From family-friendly fun to sweeping mountaintop views, discover some of the best fall activities in the Poconos at Blue Mountain Resort.
Summit Adventure Park
Don’t just watch the leaves change—climb among them at the Summit Adventure Park! Take on high ropes courses, zip lines, outdoor laser tag, and more, all surrounded by the brilliant fall foliage of the Poconos.
LEARN MORE
Scenic Lift Rides
The best way to take in the fall foliage of the Pocono Mountains is from a Scenic Chairlift Ride! Ride up the mountain on the Comet Quad, unload at the summit to enjoy the views, then head back down for a whole new perspective.
LEARN MORE
Glamping
Glamping at Blue Mountain is the perfect fall getaway! Choose from 15 canvas sites, each tucked into its own unique setting and surrounded by the colors of the Poconos.
LEARN MORE
Camping
Pitch a tent and settle in for a crisp autumn night in the Pocono Mountains. Surrounded by colorful foliage and cool mountain air, fall camping at Blue Mountain is made for memory-making.
LEARN MORE
Downhill Mountain Biking
Ride through vibrant fall scenery across 30 downhill trails. Whether you’re a seasoned rider or trying downhill mountain biking for the first time, we have ticket, lesson, and rental options to get you on the trails.
LEARN MORE
Slopeside Pub & Grill
After your adventures, unwind at Slopeside Pub & Grill with mountaintop dining and sweeping views of the Poconos in full fall color. It’s the perfect way to wrap up a fall day at Blue Mountain.
LEARN MORE
Events & Festivals
Celebrate the season with a full lineup of festivals and events on the mountain! From live music to Oktoberfest- there’s always a reason to visit Blue Mountain Resort.
