CRYSTAL SPA
Relax. Revive. Rejuvenate.
You deserve a break. Join us for year-round rejuvenation at Crystal Spa, an 18,500-square-foot oasis in the heart of Crystal Mountain. Honoring the art and natural beauty of northern Michigan, the spa awakens and inspires from the moment you enter, and will leave you feeling reset and relaxed upon exiting. Continuously ranked among Spas of America Top 100 Spas, our elegant retreat offers a variety of experiences to connect you to a healthier and more grounded version of yourself.
