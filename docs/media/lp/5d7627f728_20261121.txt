PARTNER RESORT
TELLURIDE, CO
, OPENS IN A NEW WINDOW
Discover the beauty of Telluride Ski Resort, nestled in the San Juan Mountains of Colorado. With its charming historic town and stunning peaks reaching up to 14,000 feet, Telluride offers some of the state's most rugged terrain and breathtaking views.
Pass Holders with an Epic Pass, Epic Adaptive Pass, Epic Adaptive 5-Day Pass, and Epic 4-7-Day with All Resort Access can ski or snowboard at Telluride. Visit each Pass's page for access details.
Mobile Pass on My Epic app is not accepted at Telluride. Don't forget to bring your most recent physical Epic Pass card for easy direct to lift access!
