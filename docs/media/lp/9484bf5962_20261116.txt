GO SKIING
We invite you to enjoy Skiing and Snowboarding with us here at Snow Trails in Mansfield, Ohio. Broad slopes and pristine snow conditions await you December through mid-March, thanks to major investments in snowmaking equipment. You will find our full-service Snow Trails Ski Shop on-site, plus two restaurants and bars for Après Ski in our spacious rustic Ski Lodge, slope-side. Explore this and more near Cleveland and near Columbus...

YOUR OHIO SKI RESORT ADVANTAGE:
EXCEPTIONAL SNOWMAKING W/COOLING TOWER
EXPERIENCED CREW OF SNOWMAKERS & GROOMERS
The fun and excitement of skiing and snowboarding drives our passion for great snowmaking and grooming at Snow Trails. Blanketing the slopes with fresh snow throughout the winter and meticulously grooming them, each day for you, is truly our pleasure. Our Cooling Tower is a great advantage, allowing higher quality and greater quantities of snow during each snowmaking campaign.
