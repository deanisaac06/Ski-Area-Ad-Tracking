THE TOP TIPS
1)  PURCHASE ONLINE BEFORE YOU ARRIVE TO SECURE YOUR SESSION, EVEN IF YOU'RE A PASSHOLDER. 
2)  PLAN AHEAD — GET THE MOST OUT OF YOUR VISIT BY ARRIVING EARLY. YOU'LL NEED TO ALLOW 15 MINUTES TO CHECK-IN.
3)  MAKE SURE TO CHECK ROAD CONDITIONS PRIOR TO ARRIVAL.
4)  STAY HOME IF YOU ARE FEELING SICK, HAVE SIGNS OF RESPIRATORY ILLNESS, OR HAVE A FEVER. CONTACT OUR GUEST SERVICES TEAM AND WE'LL WORK WITH YOU TO RESCHEDULE FOR ANOTHER TIME.
