MOUNTAIN IN THE MAKING
A STORY OF PEOPLE, PLACE, AND POSSIBILITY
For years, people have come to Revelstoke in search of adventure.
Some come for the skiing, others for the mountain lifestyle, and the endless opportunities to explore. But for many, what starts as a visit turns into something much more.
We're excited to introduce Mountain In The Making, a cinematic documentary series created in partnership with Sherpas Cinema, exploring the stories, people, and experiences that have shaped this mountain town into what it is today.
Told through the voices of longtime locals, adventurers, visionaries, and community members, the three-part series takes viewers on a journey through Revelstoke's history, the mountain lifestyle that draws people here, and the exciting possibilities that lie ahead.
From its humble beginnings to the vibrant mountain community it is today, REVELSTOKE. celebrates the character, authenticity, and spirit of adventure that make this place so special.
