THE MID-MOUNTAIN LODGE
Enjoy endless views at 6,700' at The Mid-Mountain Lodge. This space is perfect for dinners, galas, awards, and more. Indoor and outdoor space is available depending on the season.
Contact Our Team Here
THE ARLING CENTER
The Arling Center is located in the center of our resort close to lodging and activities. This complex hosts three different buildings and a courtyard that can be utilized for meetings, presentations, dinners, awards, and more. The rooms inside boast the latest technology perfecto for you and your team to present and share information.
Contact Our Team Here
THE LODGE AT OSPREY MEADOWS
The Lodge at Osprey Meadows is home to two venue options. The Fern and Feather is an in indoor/outdoor venue perfect for awards, conferences, dining, and presentations. It is located inside of the Lodge. The Lawn is behind the Lodge and is only available during the summer. The Lawn is right on the Osprey Meadows Golf Course and provides shade and the perfect outdoor venue.
