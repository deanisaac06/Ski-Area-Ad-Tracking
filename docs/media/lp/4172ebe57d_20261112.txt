Introducing Arclodge
Our new 73,000-square-foot clubhouse is an all-season gathering place for members and their guests.
Designed by the award-winning architecture firm Hart Howerton, the lodge is a contemporary take on a mountain clubhouse that draws on the traditions of high-alpine living. Rooted in the principles of community, connection, and a deep sense of place, the design takes inspiration from the classic grand ski lodge, but literally bends convention. Its sweeping architectural curves draw from the pristine arcs skiers trace through fresh powder, and defines this new heart of Powder Haven.
