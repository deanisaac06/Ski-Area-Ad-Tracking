Stay at the base of the mountain, in the heart of Sundance Resort.
The Inn is a 63-room hotel in the heart of Sundance Mountain Resort, offering ski-in/ski-out access just steps from the lifts, restaurants, art studios, and live music of the Resort Village. Part of a mountain experience recognized with a MICHELIN Key for three consecutive years and named the #3 Resort in the Mountain West, highest-ranked Resort in Utah, and one of America's 20 Best Ski Resort in the U.S. in the 2026 Condé Nast Traveler Readers’ Choice Awards, The Inn brings the character of Sundance Resort indoors through original artwork, unexpected objects, creekside rooms, and views of Mount Timpanogos.
Mornings begin with a European-style continental breakfast in the Living Room before the day unfolds at its own pace. Step outside to ski, explore the village, take an art class, walk beneath the trees, or spend a quiet moment beside the creek. In the evening, return to firelight, conversation, and a calm place to settle as the canyon grows still.

Explore rooms at The Inn
TIMP VIEW ROOMS
Timp View rooms place Mount Timpanogos directly outside your window, with unobstructed views of Utah's most iconic summit from morning light to evening alpenglow. Centrally located in the village, The Inn puts dining, art studios, and the ski mountain within an easy walk.

RIVER VIEW ROOMS
River View rooms sit beside the North Fork Creek, where a private deck places you at the water's edge and the sound of the stream becomes part of your stay. Dining, art studios, shops, and the ski mountain are all within easy walking distance.
 See Details
RIDGE VIEW ROOMS
Ridge View rooms look across the canyon toward the forested slopes of North Fork Ridge, the backbone of Utah's Alpine Loop scenic byway. A built-in window seat offers a quiet place to relax and take in views of the canyon that make it hard to leave the room.
 See Details
TIMP SUITE
The Timp Suite offers a spacious layout and thoughtful design built for longer stays, with unobstructed views of Mount Timpanogos, a separate living area, and the most direct view of Timpanogos that The Inn has to offer.
 See Details
CASCADE SUITE
The Cascade Suite offers expansive views of the surrounding canyon, with the North Fork Creek winding through the trees below. Inside, the suite provides room to settle in, complete with a corner seat you can nestle into with a good book.

RIDGELINE SUITE
The Ridgeline Suite looks across forested canyon slopes from one of The Inn's highest vantage points. Inside, a separate living area offers space to unwind. As the largest suite at The Inn at Sundance Mountain Resort, it offers more room to spread out than any other accommodation in the building.
