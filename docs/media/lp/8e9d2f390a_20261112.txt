START ME UP PACKAGE
LEARN FASTER WITH THE BEST STUDENT-TO-INSTRUCTOR RATIOS
New to skiing or snowboarding? Start with everything you need to build confidence and have fun on the mountain, all while saving 25% with the Start Me Up Package.
Designed for first-time and beginner skiers and riders, the package includes a group lesson, beginner-specific rental equipment, and lift access to our dedicated learning area.
Juniors (ages 6–12) learn with an incredible 3:1 student-to-instructor ratio, while Teens (13–17) and Adults (18+) benefit from a 5:1 ratio.
Great instruction, the right gear, and plenty of room to learn. Start here and get more out of your first days on snow.
LEARN MORE. STAY FOR LESS.
Book the Start Me Up Package and unlock 25% off lodging at Grand Targhee. Pair discounted lodging with your lesson, rentals, and beginner lift access for an affordable way to turn your first day on snow into a mountain getaway.
Learn to ski. Stay slopeside. Save 25%.
