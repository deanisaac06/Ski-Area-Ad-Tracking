SHARED PARENT FULL DAY LIFT TICKETS
A Shared Parent Lift Ticket is available for purchase only at Guest Services in the Village Day Lodge. This ticket cannot be combined with any other discounts.
To confirm eligibility before your visit, contact Guest Services at 250.578.5474 or email guestservices@sunpeaksresort.com.
Parents (guardians do not qualify) must have a child (age 0-12 years) who is not skiing or riding, including in The Learning Zone, and requires supervision off the mountain. 
To set up the pass, both parents and the non-skiing child must be present at Guest Services, where a photo will be taken together.
TICKET PICK UP
To redeem your ticket, present either a digital or printed copy of this order confirmation. Ensure the barcode is clearly visible on the version you present. You will then be issued a Mountain Card (RFID card).
Upon arrival at Sun Peaks, you can pick up your Mountain Card at any of the following locations:
Guest Services or Activities Desk – located in the Village Day Lodge
East Village Centre – located at the base of the Orient Chairlift
Contactless Kiosks – beside Guest Services, outside the East Village Centre, and at the base of the Burfield Chairlift. Open daily during lift-operating hours.
Save your card for future visits. Your Mountain Card can be reloaded online with additional days.
