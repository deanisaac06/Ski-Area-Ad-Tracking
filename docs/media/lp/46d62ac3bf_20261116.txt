The Sugar Bowl Wedding Experience
Rise above the ordinary in the Sierra Nevada at 8,000 feet, where the landscape meets the sky and love finds its summit. At Sugar Bowl, your rehearsal dinner, ceremony, and reception unfold across Sugar Bowl’s collection of unique mountain top, meadow, lake, and historic lodge venues. Elevated add-on experiences, like a chairlift ride to Mt. Disney, provide the canvas for an unforgettable gathering.
Gather with friends and family to clink glasses, reminisce, and take in the alpine air at the resort that was built by Austrian ski pioneers and Walt Disney all those years ago. Celebrate in a flourishing mountain meadow, beside a sparkling lake, or within our snowbound village, then elevate the experience with moments like a chairlift ride to Mount Disney for an unforgettable cocktail hour.
Rooted in heritage and surrounded by natural beauty, every wedding celebration at Sugar Bowl is designed to feel immersive, intentional, and entirely your own.

Where Unforgettable Happens
With unparalleled scenic beauty and flexibility to fit your vision, weddings at Sugar Bowl Resort are nothing short of magical. Less than 100 miles north of Sacramento and only 40 miles west of Reno, our independently-owned mountain resort doubles as the ultimate destination wedding venue. With the High Sierra as your backdrop, Sugar Bowl is ideal for both full celebrations and intimate gatherings, and perfect for a weekend spent together with your closest family and friends in a private mountain setting.
