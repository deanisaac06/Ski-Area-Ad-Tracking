Kids SKI + SNOWBOARD Lessons
Ski Lesson Packages in Berkshire County, MA
Ready to experience the thrill of skiing or snowboarding for the first time? Or returning to the slopes after some time away?
Bousquet Mountain offers beginner-friendly ski and snowboard packages designed to get you gliding with confidence. Our comprehensive packages include professional group instruction, equipment rentals, and lift access. Everything you need for a successful day on the mountain.
Whether you’re a complete first-timer looking to learn the basics or returning to skiing or snowboarding after a break, our expert instructors will help you build skills in a supportive, fun environment. Located in the heart of the Berkshires, Bousquet has been welcoming new skiers and riders of all ages since 1932. Start your snow sports journey with us today!
