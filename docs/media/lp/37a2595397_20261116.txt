WHY GO TIME TICKETS?
With Go-Time Tickets, you buy online, choose your start time, arrive, and get contactless access to the mountain. Go-Time Tickets offer ultimate freedom and flexibility while helping everyone enjoy the mountain safely. Tickets and entry may be limited at each start time, but prices gradually decrease throughout the day, so take a minute to check out Go-Time Ticket dates and times to get the best deal for you. The mountain is ready. We’re just waiting for you to tell us when “It’s Go-Time!”
Go-Time tickets must be purchased before arriving and will not be sold on-site this year.
RELOAD YOUR ONEPASS
Already have a OnePass card? Sweet! Go-Time Tickets are reloadable at RideBoreal.com/Reload. After you've chosen your date and start time and added your Group Member, you can select the "reload" option at checkout. This means future visits will be direct-to-lift, with no need to stop and grab a new OnePass card each time.
