KIDS SKI & RIDE FREE
START 'EM YOUNG, GIDDY UP!
At Steamboat, we believe in creating unforgettable family experiences on the slopes, and what better way to do that than by offering our young adventurers the chance to ski or ride for free? With our special offer, children 12 years old and under can hit the slopes alongside a paying parent, grandparent, or legal guardian. Whether your little one is a seasoned snow sports enthusiast or just starting, this program is designed to ignite their passion for skiing or snowboarding while creating lasting memories for the outdoors.
