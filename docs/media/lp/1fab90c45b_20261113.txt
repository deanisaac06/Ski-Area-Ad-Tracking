Mt. Bachelor is scheduled to open for the 26/27 winter season on December 4, conditions permitting. As always, we are committed to opening early if snowfall and conditions allow!
Mt. Bachelor's uphill policy takes effect when Mt. Bachelor opens for the 26/27 winter season, at which time a free Uphill Pass & armband are required. Until then, the slopes are uncontrolled and unpatrolled. Stay clear of snowmaking operations in the base area and on Thunderbird. There are high-pressure waterlines and high-voltage power lines between the hydrants and snowmaking guns. Call 911 in the case of an emergency. 
Mountain Gateway is open every weekend through Opening Day on December 4. Stop by the Mt. Bachelor Sports Pro Shop, featuring the PNW’s largest selection of premium ski and snowboard gear, great deals, and expert advice. Print your Season Pass at one of our PUBs, and pick up your Seasonal Rentals on the second floor.
