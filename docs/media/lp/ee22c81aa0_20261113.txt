Major Expansion Plan at Versant Soleil
The future of skiing at Tremblant is taking shape with Timber Summit, an all-new area that will enhance the mountain experience starting in winter 2027/28. This major $42.5 million project will add 62 acres of skiable terrain, eight new trails, and a high-speed chairlift, creating even more opportunities to explore the mountain. It also supports the continued growth of the Versant Soleil, notably with the confirmed arrival of Club Med Tremblant.

TREMBLANT ACHIEVES GREENSTEP LEVEL 5 CERTIFICATION
In 2026, Tremblant reached a new milestone in its sustainability journey by joining GreenStep, an internationally recognized certification program tailored to the tourism industry. Following a rigorous audit conducted by ADDERE, Tremblant achieved Level 5 certification, the highest level of recognition under the GreenStep program and a first for a Canadian ski resort.
This certification builds on more than ten years of initiatives undertaken through the former Sustainable Development Program of the Conseil des Industries Durables du Québec. The transition to GreenStep allows us to advance our approach through a program better suited to the tourism industry, strengthen our practices, and continue our sustainability efforts while setting clear objectives for the years ahead.
