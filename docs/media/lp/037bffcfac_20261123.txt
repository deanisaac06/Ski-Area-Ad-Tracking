Environmental Commitment
Telluride is known for its natural beauty and our commitment to the environment. Our resort was developed in a way that preserved the mountains and the historic charm of Telluride.
Tesla & Telluride
The connection between Telluride and Tesla is amazing. The first AC powerplant in history was designed and built by Nikola Tesla in Telluride. This innovation changed the world as we know it and allowed energy to be transported further distances than direct currents. Today, Tesla is changing history once again by leading the shift to electric vehicles.
