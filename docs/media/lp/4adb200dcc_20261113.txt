You’re Welcome Here.
Welcome to Rossland, BC—the gateway to the legendary Powder Highway. Nestled in the heart of the Kootenays, Rossland is a genuine mountain town with a fiercely independent spirit, rich history, and adventure around every corner. Discover charming cafés, independent shops, lively pubs, and a welcoming community where mountain culture isn’t manufactured—it’s a way of life.
