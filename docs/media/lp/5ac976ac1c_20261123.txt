Summer Activities at Sundance Mountain Resort
New adventures begin when the snow melts.
Sundance Mountain Resort sits in Provo Canyon, Utah, 45 minutes southeast of Salt Lake City, at the base of Mount Timpanogos, and summer here is a full season in its own right. Hike to canyon waterfalls, ride the mountain on two wheels, fly fish the Provo River, take scenic chairlift ride to panoramic Wasatch Mountains views, or zipline through the treetops. In the evenings, enjoy the Bluebird Cafe Concert Series, live music at the Owl Bar, and concerts at the outdoor amphitheater. Whether you come for the mountain or the music, Sundance Mountain Resort offers a summer experience unlike anything else in Utah.
