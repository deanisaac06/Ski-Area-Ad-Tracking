Experience the Magic of the Holiday Season
Immerse yourself in a magical winter wonderland. Beginning this December, step into a winter wonderland filled with nordic-style markets, festive celebrations, and the warmth of community. 
Whether you’re carving fresh tracks on the slopes, enjoying cozy gatherings, or savoring seasonal delights, every moment is designed to create unforgettable memories amidst the stunning beauty of Alaska’s winter landscape.

Merry Alyeska
Santa’s Stay Experience
Welcome to Santa’s stay experience at Alyeska! These enchanting holiday-themed rooms and suites are designed to capture the magic of Christmas with festive elegance and cozy charm. Step into a winter wonderland adorned with twinkling lights, red and green accents, and rich, nostalgic décor reminiscent of Santa’s Alyeska hideaway. Accommodations feature plush bedding and a whimsical, decorated Christmas tree with stunning valley views, framed by the Chugach Mountains and Turnagain Arm.
November 26, 2026 to January 3, 2027. Booking closes January 3. Room type depicted is a Signature Suite.
