SNOWMAKER
Role and Responsibilities:
Snowmakers are involved in the operation of snowmaking equipment whenever weather permits to meet our needs as determined by our Mountain Manager.
The specific duties of a Snowmaker shall include but not be limited to:                
Genuinely interested in working outside in challenging and changing weather conditions
Able to work seven (7) days per week when needed and when possible
May be asked to work another area of the resort or called off when not needed
Day shift snowmakers may be assigned to direct traffic in the parking lot on weekends and holidays
May be kept busy with other duties
Qualifications and Education Requirements:
Able to work 12-hour day or night shift in extreme weather condition
Able to frequently lift and pull a minimum of 50 pounds.
Able to pull heavy hoses or other equipment on hard packed or soft snow and on icy conditions
Must be safety conscious and aware of changing conditions
Minimum age is 18
Provide own outside clothing, warm and waterproof boots, and gloves
Dress code required
Preferred Skills:
Experience recognized but not required.
Wage: $18-$19/hr

Cascade Employee Scholar Fund
All Cascade employees, grades 9-12, who are planning to attend any form of postsecondary education are eligible to participate in the Cascade Employee Scholar Fund.  This program awards employees with additional funds on top of their regular wages to assist with the costs of postsecondary education.
Here’s how it works:
Students who complete their senior year of employment with Cascade will be eligible for a $1 donation for every hour worked through consecutive seasons during their high school career. That means if you start working here as a freshman and continue working here until your senior year, you would be eligible for a $1 donation for every hour worked for the last 4 years. More details will be given to eligible employees upon the start of the season.
