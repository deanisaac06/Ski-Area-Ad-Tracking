WINTER FIRST TIMER'S GUIDE TO ANGEL FIRE RESORT
THINGS TO DO THIS WINTER AT ANGEL FIRE RESORT
Opening Day | December 11, 2026
For skiers, snowboarders and snow lovers alike, Angel Fire Resort offers 627 acres of terrain with rolling cruisers, gladed tree runs, terrain parks and one of New Mexico’s top ski and snowboard schools.
Want to experience breathtaking summit views at 10,677 feet without skis or a snowboard? Ride our Winter Scenic Chairlift straight to the top to soak in panoramic mountain vistas.
Off the slopes, the adventure continues with our exciting tubing hill, our Nordic Center with over 15 km of terrain, and our sledding hill perfect for kids 12 and under.

KIDS' SLEDDING
ANGEL FIRE RESORT SLEDDING HILL
Located at the Nordic Center outside the Country Club is a sledding hill perfect for little ones to enjoy the snow.
We happily offer shuttle service from The Lodge at Angel Fire to the Sledding Hill from 10 AM – 3 PM when the Sledding Hill is open. Shuttle seating will be available and is offered on a first-come, first-serve basis.
