Choose more flexibility with a Refundable Season Pass.
When purchasing your 26/27 Telluride Season Pass, you can choose a Refundable Season Pass for added flexibility if your plans change. Cancel for any reason before the season starts for a 100% refund, or through February 28, 2027 for a prorated refund—the earlier you cancel, the more you get back.
Your Season Pass Must Be COMPLETELY UNUSED To Qualify For a Refund.
Cancel for Any Reason: Life, weather, injury or simply a change of plans. No covered reason is required.
No Claims or Documentation: Simply submit your refund request with Spot. No documentation or proof required.
Quick & Easy Refunds: Requests take just a few minutes, and refunds are issued in under 24 hours.
Cancel Earlier, Get More Back: Cancel before the season begins and receive 100% of your pass price back. Once the season starts, refunds begin at 90% and are prorated based on how much of the season remains. The earlier you cancel, the more you get back.
