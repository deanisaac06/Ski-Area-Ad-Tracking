OPPORTUNITIES AT WMC
Windham Mountain Club employees are dedicated professionals who take pride in delivering an exceptional level of hospitality and service to our guests. Service at Windham Mountain Club is purposeful, thoughtful, and collaborative, ensuring the quality and integrity of our brand.
Create Memories
Our team’s strength comes from a shared commitment to consistency, excellence, and care for our Members and Guests. Every employee receives comprehensive training to master their role, ensuring that they have the resources and confidence to perform at the highest standard, collaborating to make every experience our Members and Guests have a memorable one.
Call 518-734-4300 ext. 1373 or email employeeservices@windhammountain.com with any questions.
OPPORTUNITIES
Windham Mountain Club Hosts
Our volunteer Mountain Hosts are the heartbeat of Windham Mountain Club and provide the first smile and salutation to guests. Hosts earn exclusive perks, tickets, or passes depending on days of service.
Email guestservice@windhammountain.com with questions.
GUEST SERVICES
Team Housing
Windham Mountain Club offers shared/semi-private housing at multiple locations minutes from the resort. Staff housing is available for winter season and off season on a first come first serve basis. We also collaborate with existing local landlords for employee housing.
Call 518-734-4300 ext. 1102 or email employeehousing@windhammountain.com with any questions.
EMPLOYEE HOUSING
International Employment
Windham Mountain Club participates in the J-1 Work and Travel Visa Program through it’s partner, Universal Student Exchange (USE).
All interested candidates must apply through USE to complete the process.
PLEASE NOTE Windham Mountain Club does not offer an H2B program and is unable to offer employment opportunities to candidates who do not have independent U.S. work authorization at this time.
Call 518-734-4300 ext. 1373 or email employeeservices@windhammountain.com with any questions.
EMPLOYEE HOUSING
Adaptive Sports Foundation
The Adaptive Sports Foundation is always looking for Volunteer Instructors, especially for midweek lessons (Wednesday-Friday). Volunteer Instructors earn exclusive perks, tickets, or passes depending on days of service.
Email tony@adaptivesportsfoundation.org for more information and how to get involved.
