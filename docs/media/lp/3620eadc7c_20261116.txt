FUN RESORT ATMOSPHERE
Nothing quite like it around!
We truly value our employees here at Snow Trails. To show our appreciation we offer some of the greatest perks around:
FREE SKIING & SNOWBOARDING - we want you to enjoy the Resort like our guests do! Not only do you receive a FREE Associates Season Pass, but you also have the opportunity to receive free or discounted Equipment Rentals and Lessons.
DISCOUNTED MEALS - in our Cafeteria here at the Resort.
FLEXIBLE SCHEDULES -  will allow you hours of work based on your availability.
WINTER JACKETS - provided to Employees in Outdoor positions. Frequent Rotations with Indoor Warming Breaks.
Each Fall hundreds from all over North Central Ohio are accepted to work at Snow Trails for the Winter Season. Most positions fill quickly, so act fast if you would like to submit an application to become a part of our Snow Trails Team. Different departments hire at different times. Some will be making calls beginning in early October, while others will still be hiring through December. Please apply now and look to hear back from us then.
Food and Beverage Team (ages 16+)
Prepare and serve food and beverages while maintaining a clean, friendly, and efficient dining environment. Team Members ensure guests have an enjoyable experience by providing excellent service and supporting kitchen and counter operations. Indoor positions.
Guest Services and Ticket Window Team Members (ages 18+)
Serve as the main point of contact for guests, assisting with questions, Lift Tickets, Season Passes, and other Resort information. Team members provide a warm welcome and ensure smooth experiences with a focus on resort communication and support, beginning with online Bookings, to check-in, and all the way to departure. Indoor positions.
