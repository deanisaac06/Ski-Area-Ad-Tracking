Guess what all you skiers & snowboarders? The long wait is almost over... Snowbird opens tomorrow!
Resort Update:
All uphill travel is CLOSED for snowmaking and winter 2026-27 on-mountain preparation. Snowbird is scheduled to open for lift-serviced operations on November 25.
Mountain Report:
One more sleep. Opening Day at Snowbird is tomorrow, Wednesday, November 25, with first chair at 9 am.
The Aerial Tram, Little Cloud, Gadzoom, Wilbere and Chickadee are scheduled to run, conditions permitting. Check the Snowbird app tomorrow morning for the latest official lift and terrain information before heading up.
See you in the morning!

Guiding & Lessons
Guide to the goods. 
Explore more with Snowbird’s expert-guided lessons and experiences. From first-timers to advanced skiers and riders, our pros help you build skills, boost confidence and discover the best terrain Little Cottonwood has to offer—faster. Choose from private or group lessons, helicopter skiing, snowcat skiing or further your snow education with AIARE avalanche safety classes. All Snowbird lessons and guided experiences are taught by experienced instructors from Snowbird Mountain School or Snowbird Mountain Guides. Make every turn count, whether it's under the Tram or in the backcountry.
