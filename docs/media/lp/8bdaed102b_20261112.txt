RED’S SECRET WEAPON
If you’ve only skied at resorts with tiny towns attached to them, Rossland will knock you over. This Gold Rush-era “little city” with its wide, friendly main street, stone buildings, and unique shops and cafés will take you back in time. But Rossland is far more than a historic mountain town—it’s a vibrant community where outdoor adventure and small-town charm go hand in hand. After a day on the mountain, explore Rossland’s local boutiques, galleries, and breweries, all within walking distance of downtown. Whether you’re browsing the shops, enjoying live music, or swapping stories over a craft beer, Rossland has a way of making visitors feel like locals from the moment they arrive.
