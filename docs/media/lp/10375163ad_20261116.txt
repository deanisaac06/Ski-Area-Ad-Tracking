When are internships available?
Internship dates and schedules vary based on departmental needs, school requirements, and applicant availability. Most internships take place during the summer, when resort operations are busiest, though opportunities may be available at other times throughout the year.

Is Massanutten Resort employee-owned?
Yes. Massanutten's parent company is 100% employee-owned through an Employee Stock Ownership Plan, meaning eligible employees hold a stake in the company.
Does Massanutten hire seasonal staff?
Yes. Massanutten hires seasonal employees each year, particularly for ski season, alongside its year-round team. Part-time and seasonal employees also receive individual resort perks and discounts and accrue paid sick time.
