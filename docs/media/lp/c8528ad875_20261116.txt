Northern by Nature
Legendary Midwest is three family-owned resorts built around real northern winters. Lutsen Mountains sits on Minnesota’s North Shore of Lake Superior. Granite Peak rises above Wausau, Wisconsin. Snowriver Mountain Resort is tucked into Michigan’s Upper Peninsula near Wakefield. Our roots run three generations deep, and being northern is part of who we are. Whichever mountain you choose, you’ll find real winter, friendly faces, and a place that feels like home.
