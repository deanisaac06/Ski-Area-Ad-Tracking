YOUR ANGEL FIRE RESORT ACCESS CARD
This season, your lift ticket or pass is loaded onto a reusable Angel Fire Resort Access Card.
A one-time $5 media fee applies to your first card. Your Access Card is your reusable key to the mountain and will be ready for your next Angel Fire adventures, year after year.
HOW IT WORKS
Purchase your lift ticket or pass online, or at our Ticket Office.
Receive your Access Card at Pick-up Boxes near our Ticket Office and place it in your jacket pocket, with no other items inside.
Leave it there while skiing or riding. Our lift attendants can scan it right through your jacket. No paper ticket to attach to your jacket to flap in the wind, with no need to pull it out to be scanned.
WHY YOU’LL LOVE IT
Easy, hands-free lift access.
No paper ticket to manage, lose, or replace.
Keep your card for your next visit; later this season, next winter, or whenever you are ready to return.
Before your next trip, simply purchase online and your access card will be reloaded and ready to be scanned at the lift, no need to stop at the Ticket Office.
Your Access Card makes skiing and snowboarding easier and helps you spend less time checking in and more time on the mountain.

PHOTOS
Follow the guidelines below when submitting photos for your Season Pass:
Easy to recognize.
Only you are in the photo.
Head-and-shoulders view, with the entire face, both eyes and hair clearly visible; head coverings worn for religious purposes are allowed.
In focus.
No dark spots or shadows.
Black-and-white photos are acceptable.
