THE DALY
BASE AREA CONDOS WITH A POOL — BRAND NEW FOR 2026
The Daly is RED Mountain’s newest lodging, steps from the base, and the only accommodation at RED with an outdoor pool. One-, two-, and three-bedroom condos sleep 2–10, making it the go-to for families and groups who need space. Every unit has a full kitchen, in-suite laundry, and a private deck. When you’re done on the mountain, the pool, hot tub, sauna, and wellness zone are right there.
