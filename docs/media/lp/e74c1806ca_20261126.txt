Skiing and snowboarding at Powderhorn offer the perfect blend of big-mountain terrain and small-mountain charm. With 1,600 acres and 1,650 vertical feet on the Grand Mesa, riders of all levels can explore wide-open groomers, playful glades, and hidden powder stashes. Whether you’re linking your first turns or carving corduroy at sunrise, Powderhorn’s friendly atmosphere, shorter lift lines, and stunning high-desert views make every day on the snow feel like something special.
