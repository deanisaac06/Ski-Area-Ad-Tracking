BIKE SHOP
Need a flat fixed? Brakes feel a little soft? The Angel Fire Bike Shop is your one-stop full service solution. Our top-of-the-line bike mechanics will get you back on the trail in no time. The bike shop offers full gear and bike rentals, lessons, bike tune-ups and riding clinics for all ability levels. Angel Fire Bike Shop bikes are maintained by some of the best mechanics and techs in the Southwest.
