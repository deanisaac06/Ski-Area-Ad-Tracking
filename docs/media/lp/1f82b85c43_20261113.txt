WHAT’S NEW FOR THE 2026/27 SEASON
We’re getting ready for the 2026/27 season with major investments, new programs, and plenty to look forward to both on and off the mountain. From new avalanche mitigation technology and snowmaking improvements to an easier way to learn to ski or ride and another packed winter of events, here’s a look at what’s new this season. Remote Avalanche Control Technology This winter, we are introducing one of our largest investments in avalanche mitigation technology to date. Six new Gazex and Gazflex exploders at Palisades, five Wyssen avalanche towers at Alpine, and a new CATEX cable system in The Funnel will give our Patrol teams additional tools to conduct avalanche mitigation remotely. These systems are designed to help teams perform critical mitigation work more efficiently while reducing the amount of time employees need to spend traveling through avalanche terrain. They can also be operated during periods of heavy snowfall, strong winds, and limited visibility. The technology doesn’t replace the expertise and decision-making of Patrol, but gives our teams more tools to work with when preparing the mountain following significant snowfall and wind events.
