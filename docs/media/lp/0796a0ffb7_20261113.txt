A SKI HOLIDAY SHOULD BE JUST THAT: A HOLIDAY!
With over 4,400 acres of skiable terrain, some guests will ski from first chair to last laps without stopping, but that’s not for everyone. For others, a long lunch in the sun, followed by a hot tub before après is just what the doctor ordered. And you know what? That doesn’t sound too bad either.
Off the slopes, you can try snowshoeing through scenic trails, explore the wilderness on a snowmobile tour, or glide on the covered outdoor ice skating rink. Or for a more relaxed pace, hop on a horse-drawn sleigh ride or indulge in an alpine fondue followed by a starlight descent. So come to Sun Peaks for the skiing and snowboarding. And return for the Peak Moments that sent you home rejuvenated, ready to face real life once more. As a proper vacation should. Sun Peaks at your leisure.

CANADA'S SECOND LARGEST SKI AREA
4,400 acres of skiable terrain is spread across three peaks known as Tod Mountain, Sundance Mountain, and Mount Morrisey—Sun Peaks offers a winter playground with endless possibilities. Receiving an average 6 metres of snow annually, our slopes are renowned for the light, dry powder that Interior British Columbia is famous for. Each mountain is easily accessible from the central Sun Peaks village, so you can ride all three in the same day or stick to the area that you love most.

THE SUN PEAKS VILLAGE
How many resorts do you know in North America where you can ski between restaurants, cafés and shops through the village? When the clock strikes 4:00pm and the mountain bell is rung, the skiers and riders of Sun Peaks turn their attention from slopes to schoolers, as après begins.
LOCAL DINING AND SHOPPING
From sushi to schnitzel, bison burgers to grilled salmon, and homemade fudge to fresh baked goods—your taste buds will be pleasantly surprised at every turns. Shop for ski gear or browse boutique shops for apparel, locally-crafted jewellery, souvenirs and mementos, local artwork and even something for the kids.
