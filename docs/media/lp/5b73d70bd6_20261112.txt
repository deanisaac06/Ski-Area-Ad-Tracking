Come stay with us.
As northern Michigan’s premier family resort, Crystal Mountain offers a variety of lodging options that can make you feel right at home. Choose from a spacious townhome at the top of the mountain, a cottage tucked away in the peaceful woods, or a cozy hotel room at the heart of Crystal Mountain – located in the vibrant village within walking distance of restaurants and shops, plus a full-service spa and fitness center. Crystal Mountain is the perfect home away from home.
