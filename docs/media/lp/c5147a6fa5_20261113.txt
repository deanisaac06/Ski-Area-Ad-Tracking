MORE THAN A MOUNTAIN, A TRUE PLAYGROUND.
At Bromont, the mountain stretches as far as the eye can see. With 450 acres of skiable terrain across 7 mountainsides and 123 trails for all levels, including 90 illuminated for night skiing, every run is pure enjoyment. Equipped with 8 lifts, 3 magic carpets, and 9 snow parks, the mountain offers a varied and exciting playground where beginners, enthusiasts, and adrenaline seekers alike can find their thrill. For those who prefer to explore the mountain on foot, 13 alpine hiking trails allow you discover its beauty at your own pace.

Innovation Reaches New Heights
We never stop pushing the limits. Over $150 million has been invested in the mountain over the past ten years to enhance the visitor experience, modernize facilities, and expand what’s possible. Faster lifts, redesigned infrastructure, upgraded welcome areas, every project aims to make the mountain more enjoyable, efficient, and inspiring. This commitment to evolution makes Bromont a forward-looking mountain, where every visit brings something new to discover.
