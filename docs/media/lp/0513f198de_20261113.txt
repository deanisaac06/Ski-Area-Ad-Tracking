November 12, 2026 - 05:00 am
Hello! Happy Thursday November 12th.
Expect another beautiful day on the hill today with clear skies and calm winds. We should see a high of around 45 F. One more day of warm weather tomorrow and then we shift into wintery weather for the weekend.  
Hope to see you out there!
Lifts & terrain: American Eagle will be spinning at 9 AM with access to Rhapsody, Fair Play and Main Vein. Please note: NO green/beginner terrain is currently available.
Woodward Copper: An early-season hike-to park is open at the top of American Eagle.
Uphill access: Uphill travel is CLOSED at Copper Mountain. Uphill access is expected to open mid-December.
Parking & transportation: All interior lots are paid parking lots. The Flyer Lot is restricted to parking pass holders only, while the Alpine Lot is free for day skier parking. The Copper Loop and Blue Route buses run starting at 5:30 AM.
Food & beverage: Several restaurants are open for the season, including Sawmill Pizza and Taphouse, Aerie Food Hall, Downhill Dukes, plus Jack’s Bar & Slopeside Grill. Hours are available on our website.
