Frequent Skier Cards
If you plan to spend multiple days on the slopes this winter but didn't get a season pass, a frequent skier card offers the next best value!
Get your card for just $70 upfront, then ski or ride any days you choose without having to wait in the ticket line. You'll only be charged the daily rate from the table below on the days you use the card. It's a big savings compared to daily lift ticket rates and provides convenience and flexibility throughout the season.
Frequent skier cards for the 2026/27 season are available online only through Dec. 24, 2026.
Buy Now
2026/27 Frequent Skier Card Daily Rates
All frequent skier cards cost $70 upfront. Then, cardholders receive the following discounted daily rates.
Adults (ages 19-64) $88
Seniors (ages 65-69) $80
Teens (ages 13-18) $80
Juniors (ages 7-12) $45
Night Skiing (all ages) $30
Frequent skier cardholder must keep valid credit card on file for daily rates to be charged. Daily rates are charged automatically after frequent skier card is scanned at a chairlift for the first time on a given day. If first scan is before 1 p.m. cardholder is charged daily rate for their age group which includes lift access until 4 p.m. If first scan is after 1 p.m. cardholder is charged daily rate for their age group and has access through night skiing ending at 8:30 p.m. on specified days. If first scan is after 4 p.m. cardholder is charged discounted night skiing rate.
