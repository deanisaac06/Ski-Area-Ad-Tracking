Welcome to the Burke Mountain Hotel, in the heart of Vermont’s Northeast Kingdom. Located mid-mountain at the beautiful four-season Burke Mountain Resort, our hotel is the definition of ski-in, ski-out. And snowboard... and mountain bike... and hike!
Choose from a studio or a 1, 2 or 3 bedroom suite, appointed with locally-made Vermont products. Our mountain views are epic. All 116 rooms have them. You’ll soon come to learn why we call Vermont home and why we’re so happy to have you experience it for yourself.
Come ski/ride with us, bike in our bike Park and on the legendary Kingdom Trails, sit in our pub and enjoy live music, let the kids splash in the outdoor pool, have your wedding on the mountain summit.  After your first stay, we are confident that The Burke Mountain Hotel will soon become your new favorite home away from home.
