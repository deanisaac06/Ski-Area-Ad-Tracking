CHANGES FOR 2026/2027
Guests now have the option to purchase up to two RED Cards per person. RED Cards are personalized and non-transferrable, but guests can purchase up to 10 days at a deep discount by buying two RED Cards.
The 20% Locals Discount has been discontinued. The RED Card is available for anyone to purchase.  Purchase early for the best available price. For reference, the 2025/2026 Locals Rate was $559.20 for an Adult RED Card, compared to $548 when purchased during the early bird sale.
The 35% Discount off additional days has been discontinued. RED Cardholders will still receive discounts off additional days, but rates will vary based on seasonality and demand. Keep an eye out for flash sales throughout the season to add additional days at a discount.
