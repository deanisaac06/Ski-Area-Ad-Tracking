DIRECT-TO-LIFT PLUS+CARD
Link your Direct-To-Lift Plus+Card to your credit card and head straight to the lifts at Lake Louise! This free upgrade automatically bills your credit card on paid visits at Lake Louise (receipts are emailed). You also have the option to order two free Child Direct-To-Lift Cards (ages 6-12) for your kids, saving them 20% off  full-price, full-day lift tickets, and they can head straight to the lifts at Lake Louise too. Show your card at our partner resort's Guest Services desks to redeem your free days or discounted lift tickets.
*Upgrade your Classic or Digital Plus+Card to Direct-To-Lift at any time by visiting one of our locations.
RECEIVING YOUR ONLINE ORDER
A completed waiver (emailed after purchase), online profile with a photo, email address, and a credit card are required. You can collect your pass in Calgary at our downtown office, at the resort, or in Banff at the SkiBig3 Adventure Hub. Online orders cannot be picked up at partner resorts.
HOW TO USE
Show your Direct-To-Lift Plus+Card at our base area lifts to ride (Lake Louise only). Present your card to Guest Services at Castle or Panorama to redeem your free days or discounted lift tickets.
