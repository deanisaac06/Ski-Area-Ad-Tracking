SEASON PASS PERKS
Crystal Mountain Season Passholders receive perks and discounts throughout the resort and at additional ski resorts across Michigan.
PURCHASE SEASON PASSES
2026-27 Premier Unlimited Passholder Perks
First Tracks: Beginning January 2, 2027. Crystal Mountain Premier Unlimited Season Passholders plus Ultimate and Premium Snowsports Four Seasons Club Members will have early access to one chairlift on Saturdays only, beginning at 8am. Chairlift may change weekly. Check the daily Mountain Report for the weekly First Tracks chairlift schedule.
Demo Equipment Rental: Premier Unlimited Season Passholders enjoy 50% off one demo equipment rental, Monday - Friday. Excludes December 25, 2026 - January 3, 2027. Only one demo rental per season, per passholder.
Eligible for Indy Pass AddOn: The Indy Pass provides 2 days of skiing plus a third discounted day at each ski area. LEARN MORE ABOUT THE INDY PASS
Spring Season Pass Sale Bonus*: If you purchase an Adult or Senior Premier Unlimited Pass between March 10 - 20, 2026, you are eligible for two free open-to-close lift tickets to share with family and friends, valid any time during the 2026 -27 winter season. To redeem, please visit the Ticket and Rental window in the lower level of the Crystal Center. Season Passholder must be present to redeem.
*Free lift tickets only valid during the 2026-27 winter season. Season Passholder must be present with their guest to redeem their free lift ticket.wayback 
2026-27 Perks for All Crystal Mountain Passholders
Valid for all Premier and Midweek Passholders
Lodging Discount: Crystal Mountain Season Passholders enjoy great rates on select dates throughout the 2026-27 winter season. Room rates and availability vary. Book early for the best price and selection. Call (877) 430-6852 to book today.
Lift Ticket Discount: Crystal Mountain Season Passholders receive a 10% discount on Open-to-Close lift tickets when purchased at the ticket window throughout the 2026-27 winter season. No blackout dates.
Rental Equipment Discount: Crystal Mountain Season Passholders receive a 10% discount on rental equipment, with limited blackout dates: December 26, 2026 - January 2, 2027; January 16 - 17, 2027; February 13 - 14, 2027.
Snowsport Lesson Discount: Crystal Mountain Season Passholders receive a 10% discount on snowsport lessons throughout the 2026-27 winter season. No blackout dates. This discount is only available by calling the Snowsports Desk at 231-378-4776 and is not available for online bookings.
Food & Beverage Discounts: Crystal Mountain Passholders enjoy a 10% discount at the Clipper Cafe and Betsie River Pizza & Subs.
Chairlift Rides: Crystal Mountain Passholders enjoy 50% off chairlift rides during the summer and fall. Valid for one ticket per passholder, per day.
Tuning Discount: Crystal Mountain Season Passholders enjoy a 10% discount on any service in the Tuning Shop.
Retail Discounts*: Crystal Mountain Season Passholders receive 15% off retail products and softgoods. Hardgoods, groceries, beer and wine are excluded.
*Some exclusions may apply. Not valid in combination with any other discount or promotion. Please show your 2026-2027 season pass to receive your discount.
