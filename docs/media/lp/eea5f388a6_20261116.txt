WEDDING VENUES
THE ARLING CENTER & THE LAWN
The Arling Center truly does it all. This area is home to indoor and outdoor ceremony and reception venues. Choose from The Chapel, The Grange Hall, The School House, and The Lawn. The Arling Center is located in the heart of the base are of the resort just a step away from vacation rentals, dining, and activities
Explore The Arling Center Here
THE MID MOUNTAIN LODGE
Have your ceremony or reception on the mountain at Tamarack Resort. Ride the lift up and dance the day away at our newly opened Mid Mountain Lodge at 6,600' which is available to host rehearsal dinners, ceremonies, and receptions. The Mid Mountain Lodge comes equipped with one of the most stunning views of Lake Cascade and the valley below.
Explore The Mid Mountain Lodge Here
IDAHO IMPRESSIONS
Welcome to our newest venue, Idaho Impressions, located in The Village. This space is an art gallery by day and the perfect place for a cocktail party, welcome party, or a small reception by night. Curate a sophisticated vibe in the mountains. Idaho Impressions is close to the mountain and lodging making it the perfect place for your wedding festivities.
