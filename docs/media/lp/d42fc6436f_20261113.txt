This season, we’re strengthening what sets us apart.
Berkshire East Mountain Resort and Catamount Mountain Resort have refined our partnership to deliver a high-performance, two-mountain alliance defined by exceptional snowmaking, diverse terrain, and an uncompromising commitment to skiing quality.
But it doesn’t stop here. Through expanded partnerships with Burke Mountain and Smugglers' Notch Resort, your pass unlocks even more - adding destination value, variety, and flexibility across four distinctly independent mountains (see detailed benefits below).
We are Authentic Independent Mountains united by one shared commitment: to deliver exceptional experiences for our guests, both close to home and beyond.

Night Season Pass
The Night Season Pass is valid Thursday, Friday, and Saturday nights from 4pm until 9pm and Wednesdays* from 4pm until 8pm. This pass grants you access to Berkshire East and Catamount. 
*Wednesday Night Skiing is typically available in early January and ends in early March.
