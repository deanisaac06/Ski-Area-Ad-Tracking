WINTER LIFT TICKETS
WINTER LIFT TICKETS ARE ON SALE NOW!
A single lift ticket to Grand Targhee gets skiers and riders access to over 2,600 acres of exceptional terrain in the shadow of the Tetons. With a lift ticket in your pocket, 6 lifts, 2,270 vertical feet, and an average of 500+ inches of world-class Teton powder are at your fingertips. Come experience the authentic skiing and riding experience you’ve been searching for. 
The winter season runs from November 20, 2026 - April 18. 2027. Opening and closing dates are subject to change based on snow and weather conditions.

QUICK NOTES 
The Targhee Card is a reusable RFID card that is necessary to load the chairlift. Add one during checkout and pick it up at one of our Pick Up Boxes or the Activity Center. The first one is free, replacements for lost and forgotten cards are $5.
Mountain access for children 5 and under is free; however, they must have a Targhee Card. Please visit the Activity Center or Ticket Window.
Half Day Access starts at 12:30pm.
Eligible discounted tickets must be purchased in person at the Activity Center or Ticket Window. For discounted ticket types and proof of eligibility information, please see details below. 
RFID card reminders: 
Place your Targhee card or Season Pass in a pocket by itself.
Make sure your card is not near your phone, keys or wallet.
Only carry one RFID card at a time.
