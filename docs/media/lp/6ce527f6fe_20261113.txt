Mini Club
Mini Club offers a chance for 4- and 6-year-old children who are brand new to skiing to learn about their equipment, and to develop the fundamental skills necessary to learn to turn, stop, and familiarize themselves with the ski area.
Designed to foster a love of skiing, and build confidence in the beginner area of the mountain. (This is a skiing-only program and is not available for snowboarding.)
