WINTER SEASON PASSES
26/27 SEASON PASSES ON SALE NOW
Enjoy unlimited skiing and snowboarding at the ‘Ghee during the 2026/27 winter season. Grand Targhee provides over 2,600 skiable acres and a wide variety of terrain including fall line skiing, open glades, wide open groomers, and tight trees.
Your 2026/27 season pass also comes with great perks; see below for a rundown of all benefits. 
NEW FOR THE 26/27 SEASON
Every passholder, new or returning, receives one free Season Pass media card. Use it to upgrade your existing pass or keep it as a backup in case yours is lost or forgotten.
Every Season Pass comes with Pass Protection Insurance, so you're covered if life throws something unexpected your way.
School Faculty and Fire/EMS employees are eligible for 15% off their 26/27 Season Pass. Must be purchased in person. See information below for complete details. 
YOUR PASS AT WORK
We are proud that all pass proceeds go directly into resort operations, improving the skiing experience for all, and supporting our incredible employees. None of your hard-earned money is lining shareholder pockets.
