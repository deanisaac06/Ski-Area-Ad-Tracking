How can I make ski and snowboard school reservations?
+
All lessons can be booked online, but if you'd rather talk to a representative, give us a call at 801-745-3772.
Where can I rent gear?
+
Powder Mountain offers gear rentals at two of our lodges, but only the Timberline Pro Shop has the entire rental fleet.
Timberline Lodge Pro Shop: The Timberline Pro Shop offers rentals and repair services. Both Sport and Performance rental equipment can be found here.
Sundown Center: The Sundown Ski Shop is located in the night skiing area of the Sundown Center. Featured in the rental shop are Sport rentals from Salomon skis and Burton snowboards. It is the closest rental center to the Ski & Snowboard School. 
Types of Rentals:
Sport:
Sport skis and snowboards are chosen for beginner and intermediate riders, providing the perfect balance of value and quality. Ideal for those taking lessons or new to the sport, these options ensure a great start on the slopes.
Performance:
Performance skis and snowboards are for advanced riders. Our selection of high-performance brands and models curated by the Powder Mountain Pro Shop staff will have you ready for any terrain difficulty or conditions. 
Available ski brands include: Atomic, Rossignol, Black Crows, Blizzard, DPS, Stokli, WNDR, and Zag.
Available snowboard brands include: Arbor, Capita, Cardiff, GNU, LibTech, Never Summer, Roxy, Salomon, Spring Break, and WNDR
*Powder Mountain does NOT rent ski boots only.
What ability level am I?
+
First-time: you've never skied or snowboarded, or it's been many years.
Beginner: you can stop and turn on gentle green runs and are learning to link turns.
Intermediate: you link turns confidently on green and blue runs, ride the lifts comfortably, and are working on speed control and variety of terrain.
If you're between levels, choose the lower one. Your instructor can adjust, and it's better to feel comfortable than overwhelmed. 
Not sure? We'll help you choose.
Should I learn to ski or snowboard first?
+
Both are great. The best choice is the one you're most excited about.
Skiing is often easier on day one because you're upright, have two legs independent from each other, and can stop and control speed more quickly, but it can take longer to progress
