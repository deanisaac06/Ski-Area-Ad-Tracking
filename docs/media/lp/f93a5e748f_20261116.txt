FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS:
OHIO SKIING AND SNOWBOARDING LIFT TICKETS NEAR ME
Q1: WHAT IS SNOW TRAILS OPERATING HOURS?
A: Weekends and Peak Days: 9AM-9PM. Weekdays (non-Peak Days): 10AM-9PM. Lift and Terrain availability may vary based on conditions, so always check the daily Snow Report before visiting.
Q2: HOW MUCH ARE LIFT TICKETS AT SNOW TRAILS?
A: Weekends and Peak Days: All-Day Lift Tickets $73, Save $10 after 4PM. Weekdays: All-Day Lift Tickets $63, Save $10 after 4PM. Ages 6-12 Save an Additional $10. Ages 5 and under receive a Free Lift Ticket with a paying adult.
Q3: WHAT ARE PEAK DAYS AT SNOW TRAILS?
A: Peak Days include all weekends (Saturday and Sunday), school holidays, and holiday periods like Christmas week through New Year's, Fridays through Mondays of Martin Luther King Jr. Day weekend and Presidents Day weekend.
Q4: ARE THERE ANY DISCOUNTS AVAILABLE?
A: Kids ages 6-12 save $10 on Lift Tickets and Equipment Rentals. Children 5 and under receive free Lift Tickets with a paying adult and save $10 on Equipment Rentals. With Bookings required online, student, military, and senior discounts are not currently available for Daily Lift Tickets.
Q5: CAN I WALK-UP TO BUY A LIFT TICKET WHEN I ARRIVE?
A: No, all Lift Tickets are required to be booked online in advance. Walk-Up Ticket sales are not available. This policy allows us to manage maximum capacity and provide the best experience possible.
