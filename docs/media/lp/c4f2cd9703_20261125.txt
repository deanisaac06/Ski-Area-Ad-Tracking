What is the SnowCard program?
The Winter 2026/27 Panorama SnowCard includes 3 or 5 days of skiing or riding at Panorama for one low price! Plus, receive a discount off additional tickets all season long! 
The SnowCard has 3 different tiers so you can choose what's best for you and save the maximum on your ski and ride days. 
The Anytime SnowCard - For those skiing and riding over the holidays and want no blackouts.
The Restricted SnowCard - For those skiing and riding anytime including weekends but NOT over the holidays*.
The Midweek SnowCard - For those skiing and riding Monday to Friday and NOT on weekends nor holidays*.

Anytime SnowCards can be used for any date throughout the 2026/27 ski season with no blackouts.
Only one (1) SnowCard per person. Additional SnowCard purchases will not be honoured.
All SnowCard holders who use all their included days can purchase add-on days at:
15% posted ticket window rate for weekends (Saturday & Sunday) and holidays
30% off posted ticket window rate for midweek (Monday to Friday) non-holidays
SnowCards are non-refundable, non-transferable, and cannot be exchanged for a credit or any other product for any reason whatsoever.
SnowCards are only available for purchase online and by phone at 1.800.663.2929. They are not available for purchase in person at Panorama or anywhere else.
2026/27 SnowCards can be collected from Guest Services, once your pass waiver has been completed. Please see operating dates and times. 
Rates are subject to applicable taxes. Rates subject to change or cancellation without notice. Please note that the final price at checkout will be in Canadian dollars and may differ from currency conversion rates shown. Photo / age ID required at the time of pass pick-up.

About RFID Technology in the SnowCard
All SnowCards require a Panorama RFID plastic card. There is a $5 deposit for card use. All RFID plastic cards are reusable and can be reloaded with any Panorama pass. Any lost RFID cards will have a $5 replacement fee.
