Resort Update:
Uphill travel is OPEN in Peruvian Gulch only. Gad Valley remains CLOSED for snowmaking and winter 2026/27 on-mountain preparation. Snowbird is scheduled to open for lift-serviced operations on November 27.
Mountain Report:
Winter decided to make a strong appearance (a special thank you to Super El Niño for his visit to Hidden Peak). A foot and a half of new snow fell at Snowbird over the Halloween storm, turning the mountain white and giving us plenty to be excited about. Our snowmaking team is out on the mountain adding to what Mother Nature delivered as we work toward our scheduled Opening Day of Black Friday, November 27, conditions permitting.
While we are all ever-so-patiently waiting to get back on board, join us for Pre-Season Passholder Appreciation Day on November 21. Come build some preseason stoke, pick up your season pass and UDOT sticker (we are 100% back in snow tire season) and get your skis or snowboard waxed by our friends at Board Budder. Passholder Appreciation Day details
Then, carbo-load for Opening Day with Thanksgiving at Snowbird. Bring the family, make a reservation and leave the cooking and cleanup to us.
And Season Passholders, don't forget about your Early Bird Benefit (Passholder's who bought/renewed by May 13). Your free night at The Cliff Lodge is valid through December 17. The first couple of weekends of ski season are always exciting ones, so get your stay on the books while availability allows.
The mountain is white. Snowmaking is underway. Opening Day is getting closer.
