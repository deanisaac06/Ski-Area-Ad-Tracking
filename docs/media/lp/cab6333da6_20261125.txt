Winter Kickoff
winter kickoff: fondue night
the 2026-2027 winter season is here!
The Hollyburn Lodge is turning 100, and what better way to celebrate than with a special Hollyburn tradition: Fondue Night! Kick off the winter season with an evening of cozy vibes, delicious fondue, and good company. We're adding a twist this year: it’s 70s themed. Think bell bottoms, funky patterns, and all things disco. Dress to impress because there will be prizes for those with the best vibes!
december 12
6:30 pm - 10:00 pm
crazy raven bar & grill
Join us around the table with seating options for parties of two, four, or eight. Begin with starter salad, followed by classic cheese fondue served with roasted vegetables, potatoes, meatballs, and bread. To finish on a sweet note, enjoy chocolate fondue with seasonal fruit and marshmallows. A full bar will also be available to complete the experience.
