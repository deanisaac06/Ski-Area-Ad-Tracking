Lower mountain lift tickets must be purchased in-person at our Guest Services office for validation purposes.

Indy Pass
We’re happy to continue our partnership with the 2026+27 Indy Pass, a ski pass uniting 250+ of the world’s best family and independent-owned ski resorts. As a Ragged Mountain pass holder, you have the option to supercharge your pass with the “Indy Add- on Pass” to access 2 days each at every partner resort for a fraction of the price. While passes are currently off-sale for the 26+27 season, you can sign up for the waitlist to be the first to get access to the Add- on Pass if/when it goes back on sale through the summer/fall.
Sign up for the waitlist here: Indyskipass.Com
*Ragged Mountain does not require Indy Pass users to reserve in advance. Please just check-in with our Guest Services team upon arriving.
Blackout dates for Indy Base Passes:  Christmas Dec. 27-28, MLK Jan. 17-19  &  Presidents Day Feb. 14-16
