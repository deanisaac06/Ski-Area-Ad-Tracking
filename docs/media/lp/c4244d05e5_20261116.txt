Where Vows Meet I Do’s
A Peak Moment for a Lifetime Together
Tucked into the side of Saddleback Mountain, you’ll find Rangeley’s only mountainside wedding venue — a place where natural beauty and heartfelt moments come together. If the mountains are calling, Saddleback offers the picture-perfect backdrop to begin your forever.
From intimate ceremonies at the base or mid-mountain to dreamy strolls through fields of blooming lupine, Saddleback creates an unforgettable alpine experience for you and your guests.
Step into the Saddleback Lodge and be embraced by rustic elegance. Soaring vaulted ceilings, rough-hewn timbers, a grand fieldstone fireplace, and sweeping views of the Western Maine Mountains. Or take the Rangeley chairlift to The Nest at 3,620’, where floor-to-ceiling windows showcase panoramic vistas of Rangeley and Mooselookmeguntic Lakes. With sliding west-facing doors that open wide to the fresh mountain air, The Nest seamlessly blends indoor comfort with outdoor majesty, an intimate setting for your most cherished day.
