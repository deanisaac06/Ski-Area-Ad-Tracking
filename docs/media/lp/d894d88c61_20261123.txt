Experience the Magic of the Holiday Season
Immerse yourself in a magical winter wonderland. Beginning this December, step into a winter wonderland filled with nordic-style markets, festive celebrations, and the warmth of community. 
Whether you’re carving fresh tracks on the slopes, enjoying cozy gatherings, or savoring seasonal delights, every moment is designed to create unforgettable memories amidst the stunning beauty of Alaska’s winter landscape.

Merry Alyeska
Santa’s Stay Experience
Welcome to Santa’s stay experience at Alyeska! These enchanting holiday-themed rooms and suites are designed to capture the magic of Christmas with festive elegance and cozy charm. Step into a winter wonderland adorned with twinkling lights, red and green accents, and rich, nostalgic décor reminiscent of Santa’s Alyeska hideaway. Accommodations feature plush bedding and a whimsical, decorated Christmas tree with stunning valley views, framed by the Chugach Mountains and Turnagain Arm.
November 26, 2026 to January 3, 2027. Booking closes January 3. Room type depicted is a Signature Suite.

Merry Alyeska Passport
Eat, drink & be merry! Explore festive food and drink specials at five Alyeska dining destinations: aBar, Forte, Greatland, Tramway Café, and the Drink & Be Merry Christmas pop-up bar. Pick up your Merry Alyeska Passport at any participating location, collect your stamps, and turn in your completed passport at the hotel front desk for a chance to win a complimentary overnight stay at Alyeska Resort. Cheers to a season of delicious holiday cheer!

Merry Alyeska
Hospitality Gives Back Annual Toy Drive
Help make the holidays a little brighter for children and families across Alaska! As part of our Merry Alyeska festivities, Alyeska Resort is proud to participate in the Hospitality Gives Back Annual Toy Drive, organized by the Alaska Hotel & Lodging Association in partnership with Alaska Child & Family.
From November 25 through December 31, guests and community members are invited to spread a little holiday magic by donating a toy at the Alyeska Resort Hotel Front Desk. Every donation helps bring gifts, joy, and holiday cheer to children and families in need.
Join us in celebrating the spirit of giving and making this holiday season a little merrier for those who need it most!
