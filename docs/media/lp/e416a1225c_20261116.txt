26/27 SEASON PASSES
Another winter at Bolton is getting closer. Powder days, night laps, tree runs, après with friends, and those days when you show up for a few runs and somehow stay until last chair.
Whether you want to ski and ride from first chair through the night, stick to daytime laps, sneak away midweek, or earn your turns in the backcountry, there’s a pass built for the way you do winter.
And this year is a special one. We’re celebrating 60 seasons of skiing and riding at Bolton Valley, and we can’t wait to spend another one on the mountain with you.
