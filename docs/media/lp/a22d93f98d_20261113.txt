Fly Nonstop to Sun Valley From 8 Major Airports
Travel from 303 Cities Worldwide with Just One Stop
Getting to Sun Valley is easier than ever with daily nonstop flights to Friedman Memorial Airport (SUN), located just 25 minutes from Sun Valley Resort. POWDER Magazine featured Sun Valley in their roundup of top ski resorts within an hour of an airport, noting that once you land, "you can be on the mountain in record time."
Nonstop flights are available from San Francisco, Seattle, Los Angeles, San Diego, Denver, Salt Lake City, Chicago, and Phoenix. Major airlines including Alaska Airlines, Delta Connection (Operated by SkyWest), United Express (Operated by SkyWest), and American Airlines serve these routes, offering seamless connections from 303 cities worldwide with just one stop or less.

Free Parking & Transportation
Sun Valley Resort offers complimentary transportation for our guests staying at the resort from/to Friedman Memorial Airport (SUN) and the Sun Valley Resort. For more information contact Bell Service (208) 622-2122 or e-mail svshuttle@sunvalley.com. Reservations must be made to guarantee a seat upon arrival. 
Enjoy free parking at the Sun Valley Village and mountain base areas and free transportation to both Bald and Dollar Mountains and the towns of Ketchum and Elkhorn Springs via the Mountain Rides bus system. 
For more information visit www.mountainrides.org.

Discover Winter at Sun Valley
The Spa
Our signature treatments from massage therapy to salon services offer guests a variety of luxurious ways to unwind, relax, and rejuvenate.
Skiing & Riding
This is where destination skiing was born. Ski and ride on Bald and Dollar Mountains, and experience America's first destination ski resort.
Nordic Skiing & Snowshoeing
With 37 km of trails groomed daily for skate and classic skiing, the Sun Valley Nordic & Snowshoe Center provides a head-clearing experience. Ski and snowshoe equipment for all ages is available to rent at the Nordic Center located at the Sun Valley Club.
Ice Skating
The Sun Valley ice rink is a captivating year-round oasis where skaters glide under the open sky, surrounded by the majestic beauty of the surrounding mountains. 
Winter Sleigh Rides
When the snow is falling and the season is in swing, there's no better way to spend a winter day than with a sleigh ride through the hills.
Bowling
Infused with soulful 1950’s vibes and a rich history stretching back decades, the Bowling Alley and Game Room at Sun Valley Lodge is one of the Northwest's oldest bowling alleys.
Movies
Situated in the heart of Sun Valley Village, the charming Opera House was built in 1937 and serves as Sun Valley's indoor movie and performance venue.
Virtual Golf
At the Sun Valley Club, golfers can enjoy year-round virtual play on over 100 world-renowned courses, ideal for both individual practice and group gatherings.
