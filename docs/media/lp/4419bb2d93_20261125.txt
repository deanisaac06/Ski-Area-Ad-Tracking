Guess what all you skiers & snowboarders? The long wait is almost over... Snowbird opens tomorrow!
Resort Update:
All uphill travel is CLOSED for snowmaking and winter 2026-27 on-mountain preparation. Snowbird is scheduled to open for lift-serviced operations on November 25.
Mountain Report:
One more sleep. Opening Day at Snowbird is tomorrow, Wednesday, November 25, with first chair at 9 am.
The Aerial Tram, Little Cloud, Gadzoom, Wilbere and Chickadee are scheduled to run, conditions permitting. Check the Snowbird app tomorrow morning for the latest official lift and terrain information before heading up.
See you in the morning!

Stay at Snowbird.
Experience the best of Utah hotels with Snowbird’s mountain-side lodging, just steps from world-class ski terrain and stunning alpine views. Whether you’re here for a winter ski trip or a summer mountain escape, Snowbird's lodging puts you closer to the action, and to every unforgettable sunrise over Little Cottonwood Canyon.
Choose from a variety of options, including full-service hotel rooms with extravagant dining and rooftop spas, to cozy condos with kitchens and spacious living areas. Staying within the Snowbird Village means you're waking up closer to first Tram, enjoying après steps from your room and relaxing in comfort when the lifts stop spinning.
