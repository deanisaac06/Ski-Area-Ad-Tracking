Kids Ski Free is back for the 2026-27 season!
KIDS SKI FREE AT MARMOT BASIN
Bring the whole family to Marmot this season and save! From Opening day-December 18, 2026, kids ski and rent free! After January.3 2027, All-season long, Monday-Friday all kids 12 and under ski and ride FREE! Not only that, they also get free rentals.
WHAT'S INCLUDED:
Free lift tickets. All kids 12 and under ski and ride completely free.
Free rentals. Skis, poles, boards, boots, and helmets are on us.
Make family memories this season without breaking the budget! Head to our ticket windows on arrival at the mountain to get your free junior lift pass and then present it to our rentals desk to take advantage of your free perks.
*Blackout dates are December 19, 2026 - January 3, 2027
VACATION CAMPS
This season our Vacation Camps are back! These ski or snowboard programs are designed to help your kids improve their skills and confidence. Whether they're brand new to the sport or looking to refresh their skills, our instructors will help them build a solid foundation and the confidence to shred the mountain all winter.
