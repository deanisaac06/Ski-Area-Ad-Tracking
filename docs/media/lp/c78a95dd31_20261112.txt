WELCOME TO LOVELAND SKI AREA
Near the hearts of Colorado skiers and riders for nearly 90 years, family-owned Loveland Ski Area is Colorado’s true winter home. Just 53 miles west of Denver, Loveland offers 1,800 acres of legendary terrain, FREE close-in parking, and FREE snowcat skiing along the Continental Divide. With an innovative Ski & Ride School program, more snow than any Front Range or Summit County resort, and one of the snowiest, longest seasons in North America—late October through early May—Loveland delivers the best deals in Colorado on season passes, lift tickets, and lessons, keeping skiing and riding accessible, affordable, and authentic. No crowds. No hassles. Just Colorado’s pure winter experience.
