ULLR Fest 2026
ULLR FEST 2026
SATURDAY, DECEMBER 05 2026
7:00 PM - 10:00 PM
Join us as we honor ULLR, the Norse God of Snow, and summon a season full of powder! Gather around the roaring bonfire for the time-honored ski and snowboard sacrificial burning — bring your old gear and toss it into the flames as we cheer for epic winter adventures.
Don’t have any old gear to sacrifice? We’ve got you covered! We’ll have wooden skis and snowboards available to toss into the flames in exchange for a donation to Protect Our Winters. Plus, $1 from every shot of ULLR sold will go toward supporting their mission to “depoliticize the climate discussion and bring together those from all backgrounds to advocate for the outdoor spaces we love most.”
Warm up with our favorite Viking pastime, mulled wine! Bring your own cup or snag a limited-edition Alyeska cup, and raise a hearty toast to ULLR. Want to look the part? Grab a light-up Viking hat and join the festivities!
The Best Fest Dress Contest is BACK! Don your Viking hats, furs, cloaks, and jewels for a chance to win a 2027/28 ALYESKA SEASON PASS. Outfits must feature at least three pieces of flair to compete. No weapons allowed (real or imaginary…we’re looking at you, trolls and Vikings!).
This event is free to the public, and all ages are welcome!
The show at the Sitzmark kicks off at 10:00 PM; show is for 21+ only and requires a ticket for entry. Grab your tickets for High Step Society here! 
SCHEDULE OF EVENTS:
7:00 PM Event Kick-off & Best Fest Dress Contest Registration Opens
8:00 PM Best Fest Dress Contest (must register by 7:45pm)
8:30 PM Pop-up Performance
9:00 PM Raise a Glass to ULLR
10:00 PM Live at the Sitz: High Step Society | $30
