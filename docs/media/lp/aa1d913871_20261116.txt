EMPLOYMENT
Submit your application today! Hiring for winter positions will start in late October. Take a look below for positions we are hiring for at the Ski Area!
PERKS:
Trollhaugen Season Pass (must remain active/ 2 shifts/week) 
Free skiing for ONE friend or IMMEDIATE family members on Wednesdays, including free rentals 
10% OFF Ski Shop Purchases 
Discounts on Snack Bar Food/Drinks while working
REQUIREMENTS:
All employees are required to consent to a background check.  Previous conviction history does not automatically disqualify you from employment.
We are open on Christmas and New Year’s – please expect to work at least one shift on one of these days. 
Must be able to smile and talk with guests in a friendly manner.  Outgoing personalities are a ++ 
PLEASE NOTE: Nearly all employees will interact with guests and must be ready to do so in a positive, outgoing, and cheerful manner.  Individuals who are not interested in smiling, holding open doors for others, or keep the ski area clean need not apply.

Base Host: 18+: Work outside and talk with guests! This position must be friendly and be will speaking with guests as the main part of their job.  You might be checking tickets, greeting guests, or getting skiers and snowboarders to gather into groups of 4s to go up the chair. Starting Wage $17.00-$19.00

Snack Bar Cashier: 18+, fast food style service, take orders, run cash register. No experience required, all shifts available. Starting Wage $15.00
Snack Bar Cook: 16+, fast food style cook, must be a quick worker. No experience needed, all shift available.  Starting Wage $15.00-$17.00

Bartenders: 21+, Experience preferred, looking for weekday and weekend shifts. Must be able to work alone and be a self starter during slow times. Starting Wage $9.00+tips

Experience mounting/waxing skis is a bonus but we will train!
