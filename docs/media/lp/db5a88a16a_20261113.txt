ANGEL FIRE RESORT LODGING
Angel Fire Resort offers several lodging options to relax and get a good night’s rest after a long day in The Moreno Valley and The Enchanted Circle.
When you stay at Angel Fire Resort, you have access to many amenities:
Stay in the closest accommodations to the lift
Free WiFi internet access (Some properties may not have internet)
Multiple dining options available in The Lodge and base area
24-hour front desk and security
No booking fees
On-call maintenance for unforeseen issues
THE LODGE
The Lodge is steps from the Chile Express chairlift and located in the heart of Angel Fire Resort’s base area. Take a plunge in our indoor pool, enjoy a relaxing soak in our hot tub, sweat it out in the fitness center, or browse the internet with our free WiFi access.
Conveniently located on each floor are a washer and dryer. Pet-friendly rooms are available.
BOOK NOW
CONDOS AND HOMES
We offer many private condos and homes for rent near the mountain, Monte Verde Lake and the golf course. Our 1, 2, 3, and 4 bedroom fully-furnished vacation rentals combine spacious living with spectacular scenery and are perfect for families and groups looking for privacy.
BOOK NOW
ANGEL FIRE RV RESORT
Looking for a place to park your home away from home? Angel Fire RV Resort is a luxury RV resort situated near Angel Fire Resort. It is the perfect location for your home base while you get out and explore the natural beauty of the Enchanted Circle area.
