SCENIC CHAIRLIFT RIDES
2026 LIFT SCHEDULE & TRAIL STATUS
Enjoy Angel Fire and the Moreno Valley like never before on the summer’s longest, fastest high speed quad (over 2 miles long!) chairlift in the world. Take in the beautiful mountain views as our scenic chairlift whiskes you 2000 vertical feet to the mountain summit at 10,677 feet. Once at the top, you can enjoy disc golf and hiking, and enjoy lunch with a view from the Summit Haus.
When you are ready to head back, you have the option of either hiking down or riding the chairlift back to the base area. The views on the way down allow you to see the entire valley, Wheeler Peak, and Eagle Nest Lake State Park.

LIGHTNING POLICY
During monsoon season thunderstorms with lightning are likely to occur every afternoon. When we experience lightning, this is what happens with the chairlift:
When lightning strikes are detected within 30 miles, we begin closely monitoring the radar.
At a 20-mile range, we stop allowing foot passengers to load the chairlift at the base until there have been no strikes for 30 minutes. (Mountain bikers may continue to load, as they don’t rely on the lift to return to the base.)
If a strike occurs within 10 miles, the chairlift will close to all passengers and be shut down until there are no strikes for 30 minutes. (Foot passengers already at the top may have to wait up to two hours for either a shuttle or the lift to resume operation after the lightning hold.)

Safety and Instructional Info:
If unfamiliar with use of lift, please ask operator for instructions
This is a moving lift – the chair will not stop for loading/unloading unless you specifically ask the operator to stop it or slow it down for you
Passengers must be at least 32 inches tall to ride the Chairlift
Children must be able to sit on the chair without assistance from parent; children are not allowed to sit in laps
Closed toed shoes are recommended
Pets, Emotional Support Animals, and some Service Animals are not permitted to ride the Chairlift – visit our Accessibility page for more info
Additional instructions, safety tips, and rules: Chairlift Rides – Rules and Instructions
Angel Fire Resort staff will have the final determination on anyone loading the lift due to various safety concerns
Please note that 3:30 PM is the latest time foot traffic can ride up the Scenic Chairlift.
