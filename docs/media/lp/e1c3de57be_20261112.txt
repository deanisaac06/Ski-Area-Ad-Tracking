How do I pick up my Powder Mountain Pass?
+
Check your purchase confirmation email. It includes a voucher with a QR code. Just scan the QR code at any Ticket Pickup Box located outside our three lodges. Now, you are all set!
How are you planning to stay uncrowded in 2026/27?
+
Limited Day Tickets
For the 2026/27 season, we’re continuing to focus on maintaining an uncrowded experience by limiting Day Ticket sales and prioritizing our Season Passholders.
No Mega Passes
The Indy Pass will no longer be accepted at Powder after the 2023/24 season.
Epic and Ikon passes are not accepted at Powder.
What is the parking plan for the 2026/27 season?
+
To mitigate crowding and incentivize carpooling, we will continue paid parking on weekends and holidays. All parking lots will follow the parking plan below. A Powder Mountain Season Pass or Lift Ticket grants you free access to the UTA Ski Bus.
