KNOW BEFORE YOU GO
Skiing and riding doesn’t have to be complicated—we’re here to make your experience as smooth and enjoyable as possible. Below, you’ll find money- and time-saving tips to help you get the most out of your visit to Bousquet!
Ticket kiosk
Using our handy ticket kiosks, you can scan the barcode from your confirmation email, your lift ticket will print out, and you can hit the slopes! 
RFID CARD
Your RFID card is yours for life—hang onto it and save $5 every time you visit Bousquet! 
MILITARY DISCOUNT
Active and retired military members get 10% off 4- and 8-hour lift tickets and rentals for themselves and immediate family. Offer available at the ticket window with valid military ID.
Night skiing
Night skiing at Bousquet Mountain is the perfect way to end your day! Conveniently located in the heart of the Berkshires, we’re just minutes away—making us the best spot to ski or ride after work or school. Grab a Twilight Lift Ticket and enjoy an evening on the slopes under the lights!
