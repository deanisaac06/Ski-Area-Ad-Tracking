Intimate Weddings. Incredible Setting.
Celebrate your wedding in a more personal, intentional way—without sacrificing the beauty of a mountain resort setting. At Blue Mountain, our Valley Weddings are designed for couples who value meaningful moments, scenic surroundings, and a simplified planning experience. If you’re searching for an affordable wedding venue in the Poconos that still delivers a one-of-a-kind backdrop, you’ll find it here at Blue Mountain Resort.

The Valley Experience
Nestled in the Valley portion of Bue Mountain, this setting offers a unique perspective with sweeping views looking up at our mountain landscape, a seamless indoor/outdoor venue experience, and a more private atmosphere. You’ll enjoy:
A spacious private indoor venue complete with a bar and dance floor
Outdoor ceremony and reception spaces surrounded by mountain lanscape
Expansive open fields for outdoor celebrations and gatherings that are fully customizable
All the beauty of a mountain resort wedding in a setting that feels more personal.
