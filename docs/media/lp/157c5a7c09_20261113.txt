Come Ski With Us
You don't come here to escape your life. You come here to remember what you wanted it to feel like. Whether you're planning your first visit or returning for another winter, everything you need to plan your trip is right here. Book ahead to get the best value on lift tickets, rentals, and lessons—and get on the hill faster.
Lift Tickets
At Aspen Snowmass, a single lift ticket unlocks 5,700+ acres of terrain across four unique mountains. Save up to 25% on lift tickets when you purchase 30 days in advance.
Lessons
Our expert Ski & Snowboard Pros are your guide to having the best day on our mountains. Secure the best price when you purchase group or private lessons ahead of time, all season long.
Rentals 
Save 20% on rentals when you book 30 days out, or 10% at seven days. Nine Four Mountain Sports locations make it easy to get dialed in—then your gear follows you, stored overnight and ready at whichever mountain you ski next.

Lodging in Aspen Snowmass
Stay Aspen Snowmass
Stay Aspen Snowmass offers lodging for every lifestyle, from elegant comfort to family-friendly warmth. Browse the best selection of local accommodations on your own or let our friendly vacation advisors plan your next trip.
The Little Nell
Aspen's only Five-Star, Five-Diamond, ski-in/ski-out hotel features luxurious amenities, personalized service, breathtaking mountain views, renowned dining, and spacious, pet-friendly accommodations.
Limelight Hotels
Downtown Aspen or slopeside at Snowmass, pick your playground. Limelight Hotels offer community lounges to linger, rooms for you and your pup, breakfast that fuels first tracks, and après for story swapping.
