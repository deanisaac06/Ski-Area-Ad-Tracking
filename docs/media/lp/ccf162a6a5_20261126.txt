WOODWARD RESERVATION HUB
SESSION PASS + MEMBERS: RESERVE YOUR WOODWARD SESSIONS
Woodward Bunker Session reservations are required for Woodward Members or monthly Summer Sessions Pass holders. Log in to your account in the online store, then head to the Woodward Reservation Hub to secure the session time(s) you want for your 2-hour session inside The Bunker. This reservation system ensures you, a passholder or member, have the access you want, when you want it, before daily session times sell out. You can reserve up to 10 daily sessions same-day or weeks in advance; the flexibility is yours. Come when you want, and reserve your time online.
WHAT IS A SESSION?
We're all about experiences at Woodward Tahoe, whether in a lesson or just navigating through zones on your own. Access to The Bunker and our facilities is limited to a certain number of participants to provide the best experience possible. Access is split up into two-hour 'sessions', where participants can take advantage of all parts of The Bunker, like the trampolines, skatepark, or foam pits. Participants can also book into multiple sessions in the same day (pending availability) if they need extra time to land that trick, or if they are having too much fun to stop.
