ABOUT WELCOME TO SUNDANCE MOUNTAIN RESORT
Part haven for the arts, part mountain playground, and part nature conservation, Sundance Mountain Resort invites you to experience Utah’s Wasatch Mountains at a different pace. In autumn, the mountain turns gold and amber as art, seasonal flavors, and time outdoors come together beneath Mount Timpanogos.
This year, Sundance Resort is honored to receive One MICHELIN Key for the third consecutive year and to be named the #3 Resort in the Mountain West, highest-ranked Resort in Utah, and one of America's 20 Best Ski Resort in the U.S. in the 2026 Condé Nast Traveler Readers’ Choice Awards. Together, these recognitions celebrate the thoughtful hospitality and distinct sense of place at the heart of Sundance, and the guests who continue to choose this mountain year after year.
Less than an hour from Salt Lake City and Park City, settle in for crisp mountain mornings, days outdoors, evenings by the fire, and the quiet transition from fall to the first snow of winter.
