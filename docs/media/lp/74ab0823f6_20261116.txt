FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS: OHIO SKI RESORT NEAR ME
Q1: WHERE IS SNOW TRAILS LOCATED?
A: Snow Trails is located at 3100 Possum Run Road in Mansfield, Ohio, just 1.5 miles off I-71. We're conveniently located near Cleveland and near Columbus, easily accessible for day trips from both cities.
Q2: WHAT ACTIVITIES ARE AVAILABLE AT SNOW TRAILS?
A: Snow Trails offers skiing, snowboarding, and snow tubing. We have 21 trails for Skiing and Snowboarding, five terrain parks, and the Vertical Descent Tubing Park featuring the longest Tubing Lanes in Ohio at 1,100 feet.
Q3: WHEN IS SNOW TRAILS OPEN FOR THE SEASON?
A: Our season typically runs from December into March, weather permitting. Opening dates depend on Snowmaking conditions, with operations generally beginning in the second week of December. Check our Snow Report for current conditions and hours.
Q4: DO I NEED TO BOOK TICKETS IN ADVANCE?
A: Yes, bookings are required in advance online for both Lift Tickets and Tubing Tickets. Walk-Up Ticket sales are not available. We recommend booking early, especially for Peak Days (weekends and holidays), as Tickets often sell-out.
