Does Massanutten Resort offer package deals?
Yes. Massanutten offers seasonal packages and specials across all lodging types, updated regularly. Book online or call 540.289.4952 for current offers.
Is there a military discount at Massanutten?
Yes. Active duty, veterans, and their families receive a discount on eligible lodging bookings with a valid military ID at check-in.
What kinds of packages and specials does Massanutten offer?
Massanutten Resort packages may include seasonal lodging deals, Waterpark offers, golf packages, ski savings, holiday specials, and limited-time resort discounts. Available offers change throughout the year.
Do Massanutten Resort packages include activities?
Some packages may include activity discounts, Waterpark access, resort gift cards, or other special benefits, while others are lodging-only. Review each package for current details, inclusions, and restrictions before booking.
Can packages and specials be combined with other offers?
No. Packages and specials generally cannot be combined with other offers or discounts.
Is there a minimum stay for Massanutten lodging packages?
Many condo rental packages require a two-night minimum stay. Minimum stays, eligible dates, and available lodging types may vary by offer.
How do I book a Massanutten Resort package?
Packages can typically be booked online or by calling the Rentals Department at 540.289.9441. If booking by phone, mention the package name to receive the listed package price.
