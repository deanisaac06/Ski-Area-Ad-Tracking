SKIWEE & SNOW EXPLORERS
BOOK SKIWEE AND SNOW EXPLORERS
Winterplace Ski Resort is all about family fun for all ages. SkiWee & Snow Explorers learn the basics in a private learning area adjacent to the main resort center. This specialized instructional program provides the best learning environment for the children to learn to ski or snowboard. SkiWee & Snow Explorers uses a group format dividing children by age and ability. This 1/2 day program will provide your child with the basics for a lifetime of memories and FUN! $155 in advance // $165 walk-up. (Multi-day options available at checkout)
SkiWee & Snow Explorers Program is a really big, fun time for the youngest skiers and riders in the family, ages 5 through 12. All Day Lift Ticket, Equipment, Helmet, Supervision, Instruction, and LOTS OF FUN are all included! 
Hours of Operation: 
AM Session 9:00am - 12:00pm
PM Session 1:00pm - 4:00pm
Group sizes range from 4-8 children per instructor. The program is appropriate for children ages 5-12 years of age. SkiWee & Snow Explorers emphasizes having fun while learning. We want your child/children to love the sport! 
Reservations are required at least 24 hours in advance. If space allows, late reservations will be permitted.
Parents - SkiWee & Snow Explorers is easy as 1, 2, SKI!
Come rested and fed! Breakfast is very important.
Bring stuff to wear to stay warm and dry!
Upon arrival - check-in at the Skiwee & Snow Explorers Building (the first building to the left of the resort center.)
You will then leave your child/children for FUN on the snow!
Then off you go-skiing!
Parents and Children - Helpful Tips - Come prepared to be out on the snow for the majority of the session. Dressing in layers that fit and that are waterproof is important. Protective eyewear is strongly recommended, i.e. sunglasses or goggles along with sunscreen for those sunny days. Please remind your child that it is important to listen to their instructor, be patient and have FUN!
