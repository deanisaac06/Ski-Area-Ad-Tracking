Purchase
Tickets can be purchased online, by phone at 1-866-356-2247 (toll-free) or on site at the multiservice centre (except for Prestige zone tickets not available on site)
Advance purchase is strongly recommended.
Children ages 0 to 4 do not require a ticket.
Maximum of 5 Prestige tickets per guest.
Royalty and taxes extra. Rates, royalty and taxes are subject to change without notice.
Delivery
General Admission and PwC Fan Zone tickets: You will receive an email containing your electronic tickets within a few minutes of purchase. Go directly to the access gates at the base of the mountain, either on foot via Rue des Remparts or via the Cabriolet lift, located across from the Chalet des Voyageurs. Present your QR code at one of the entrance gates and an admission wristband will be provided on site.
Prestige tickets: You will receive communications before the event. Head to the base of the Cabriolet lift and use the Prestige Priority Access line. Then, make your way to La Forge restaurant, located at Place Saint-Bernard, to pick up your admission wristband and VIP parking pass.
