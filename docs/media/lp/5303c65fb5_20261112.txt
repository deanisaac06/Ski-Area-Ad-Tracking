GOLF SIMULATOR
Indoor Golf Simulators are only available during the winter season.
Golfers, keep your swing sharp all winter long! Crystal Mountain is excited to offer winter golf with two high-tech TrackMan simulators.  Open to all ages and skill levels, you can play over 400 virtual courses for fun or book professional lessons and club fittings. 
This is a fantastic way to keep your swing loose, work out those pain points from last season, and simply enjoy golf regardless of the snow outside. Now, guests can ski and tee off on the same day. It’s time to hit the virtual links!

About Your Booking
Simulator Bays
We have two golf simulator bays available in the Kinlochen Shop. Each bay comfortably accommodates up to four golfers at a time. 
Plus, you can watch live sports while using the simulators and enjoy convenient food and beverage service from The Thistle right from your bay!
Cancellation within 48 hours will forfeit the full amount.  All participants must sign a waiver.  For questions contact the Kinlochen Pro Shop at 231-378-2049. 
Simulator Time
As a general guideline, one person can play 18 holes in an hour, while two people can get through 9 holes in that same timeframe.
Hours of Operation
Simulators are open from 9am - 9pm, with the final golf simulator rental starting at 8pm. Simulators are available to rent beginning November 28, 2026 through April 4, 2027.

How It Works
Flexible Weekly Scheduling: Life is busy, so we make league play easy. You and your partner can book your simulator hour whenever it fits your schedule each week—you don't even have to play at the exact same time as your opponents (though head-to-head matchups are always welcome if schedules align!).
Round-Robin Regular Season: Play against all 10 teams throughout the season. Holiday weeks are automatically skipped so you never have to stress about scheduling around the holidays.
The Thistle: Every weekly round is meant to be enjoyed, so grab some food and beverages from The Thistle while you play.
