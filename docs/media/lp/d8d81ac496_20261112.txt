Say “I do” at Blue
The Summit at Blue Mountain Resort offers an unmatched setting for your wedding day. Perched at the top of the mountain, this one-of-a-kind venue surrounds your celebration with breathtaking views of the Pocono Mountains. From your ceremony to your reception, every moment feels elevated in a setting as unforgettable as the occasion itself—paired with exceptional hospitality, thoughtful service, and curated food and beverage experiences designed to bring your celebration to life.

The Ceremony Site
“I Do” with a view
The view speaks for itself. Say “I Do” with a sweeping backdrop of the Pocono Mountains in every direction. Our summit ceremony site is unlike anything else in the region and delivers an unmatched backdrop for your big day. From your walk down the aisle, to your first kiss, every moment will be framed by the mountain.

The Ballrooms
Blue Mountain offers two elegant mountain-top reception ballrooms. Each room features floor-to-ceiling windows with panoramic mountain views. Choose one ballroom or both for a private experience.
Vista Ballroom
Our Vista Ballroom offers a dramatic view of the Pocono Mountains. With 4,500-square-foot event space, sloping wood-paneled ceiling, and stylish chandeliers this is the perfect location to celebrate your vows.
VIEW VISTA GALLERY 
Alpine Ballroom
The Alpine Ballroom boasts an exposed rafting ceiling, large bar, natural light, and beautiful views of the Pocono Mountains as well as our summit courtyard. This space is 5,400 square-feet.
