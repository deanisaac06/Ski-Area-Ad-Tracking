Experience Lookout Pass
Where Tradition Meets Adventure
Lookout Pass is more than just a ski resort, it’s a legacy destination straddling the border of Idaho and Montana, where every kind of snow lover can find their thrill. This unique location gives visitors stunning views, diverse terrain, and a true mountain experience unlike anywhere else in the Northwest.
Known for its welcoming, family-friendly atmosphere and challenging terrain for seasoned skiers and riders, Lookout Pass offers something for every level of adventurer. With 1,023 skiable acres, 59 trails, a 1,650 ft vertical drop, 3 terrain parks, gladed tree runs, and over 450 inches of snowfall each year, Lookout Pass consistently delivers some of the lightest and deepest powder in the region.
