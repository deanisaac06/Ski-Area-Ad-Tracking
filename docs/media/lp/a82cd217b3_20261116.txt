SOLITUDE CANCELLATION POLICY
THE INN AT SOLITUDE
First night room and tax required as a deposit. The remaining amount is due 14 days prior to arrival. Cancellations made within 72 hours after booking will be refunded in full. Cancellations occurring more than 72 hours after booking, but prior to 14 days before arrival, will forfeit the deposit. Cancellations made within 14 days of arrival will forfeit the full amount. No refunds for no-shows, late arrivals, or early departure.
 CONDOS, TOWNHOMES, PRIVATE HOMES, VACATION RENTALS
First night room and tax required as a deposit. The remaining amount is due 21 days prior to arrival. Cancellations made within 72 hours after booking will be refunded in full. Cancellations occurring more than 72 hours after booking, but prior to 21 days before arrival, will forfeit the deposit. Cancellations made within 21 days of arrival will forfeit the full amount. No refunds for no-shows, late arrivals, or early departures.
SKI & RIDE, RENTALS
We will gladly give you a full refund if you cancel before 5 p.m. two days prior to your reservation; otherwise, a $20 fee per reserved product will apply. No refunds will be given for day-of cancellations. No-shows for any reserved services will be charged the full-day rate for the missed days.
TICKETS
Lift tickets are non-refundable (including for weather concerns), non-transferable, and valid only for the date issued. Multi-day tickets are valid for consecutive dates.
