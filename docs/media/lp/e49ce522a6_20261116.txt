FINDING THE RIGHT MT SEYMOUR LESSON
We offer ski and snowboard programs tailored to every age and skill level on the mountain. Explore our core programs below:
Tots & Kids Lessons (Ages 3-12): Fun, safety-focused group environments built to establish strong fundamentals on our dedicated beginner terrain through to advanced skill development with our multi-day lessons and camps 
Teens & Adults (Ages 13+): We offer both beginner discovery packages through to advanced terrain park clinics.
Private Lessons (All Ages): One-on-one coaching designed to rapidly accelerate your progression.
