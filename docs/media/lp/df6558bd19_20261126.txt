JUMP START YOUR PROGRESS
Learn to ski or snowboard at Boreal Mountain Resort in Lake Tahoe. Our award-winning instructors are proud recipients of the NSAA Conversion Cup, recognizing us as the Best Place in the Country to Learn to Ski & Snowboard. Whether it's your first day skiing or snowboarding or you're leveling up your skills, we make learning fun, fast, and confidence-building.
Students of similar skiing and snowboarding skill levels will be grouped up with a trained instructor to set lesson goals, develop skills, and build confidence, all while enjoying the support and encouragement of your fellow peers. Starting at age 4, Boreal group ski and snowboard lessons are 90 minutes long and are designed for First Timer, Beginner, and Intermediate skiers and snowboarders.
Also, take a look into our Take 3 Ride Free program, one of the best ski and snowboard lesson deals in Lake Tahoe! Take 3 Group Lessons and get a FREE Season Pass!
