Finish Planning Your Winter
Ski and Ride School
Whether you’re a first-timer or want to hone in your expert technique, Snowbasin Resort’s talented instructors will assist you in meeting your skiing and riding goals in a fun environment.
Rentals
Top-notch ski & snowboard equipment at your convenience. Open daily during the ski season and located slopeside, right at the bottom of the hill for easy pick up and drop off.
Learn & Earn
For first-timers ages 4 and up, Learn & Earn turns a first winter into something bigger. Group lessons, season-long rentals, and a Premier Season Pass help you go from first turns to exploring the mountain, with the option to keep progressing for up to three seasons.
Multi-Week Youth Programs
Seasonal programs for young skiers and riders who already have some experience on the hill. Kids come back week after week to build skills, confidence, and friendships with the same instructor and group, with options ranging from our Busters programs to the new Basin Freestyle Club in the terrain park.
