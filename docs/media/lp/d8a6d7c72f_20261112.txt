Frequently Asked Questions
Do kids ski free?
Kids 6 and younger always ski free — but they do need to have a lift ticket.
How do multiday tickets work?
Multiday tickets include a one-day grace period beginning with your preselected start date. For example, you could use a three-day ticket to ski Monday, Tuesday and Thursday, using Wednesday as a rest day. Multiday tickets are for regular operating hours only and don’t include the swing shift or night skiing.
Are there ticket refunds?
Requests for lift ticket refunds must be made at least 48 hours prior to the ticket start date.
