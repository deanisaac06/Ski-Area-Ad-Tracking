QUESTIONS ABOUT LESSONS?
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS:
OHIO BEGINNER SKIING AND SNOWBOARDING LESSONS NEAR ME
Q1: WHAT IS INCLUDED IN THE LET'S GET STARTED PACKAGE?
A: The Let's Get Started package ($110, value $145) includes: Beginners Area Lift Ticket, Ski or Snowboard Equipment Rental, and a 1-hour Group Lesson. It's designed for ages 9 years and up who have never skied or snowboarded.
Q2: HOW SHOULD I PREPARE FOR MY LET'S GET STARTED SESSION?
A: Arrive early to: 1) Get your Equipment at the Rental Center, 2) Practice sliding in the Terrain Based Learning Park, 3) Get comfortable with being on your Skis/Snowboard, 4) Check-in at Snow Sports Window 15 minutes before your Lesson time.
Q3: WHAT IF I'M UNDER 9 YEARS OLD?
A: The Let's Get Started package is for ages 9 years and up. Children ages 4-9 should register for our all-inclusive Children's Polar Programs, which include supervision, instruction, lift ticket, equipment, and snacks.
Q4: CAN I CONTINUE SKIING AFTER MY LESSON?
A: Yes! Your Beginners Area Lift Ticket is valid all-day. After your Lesson, you can continue practicing on the gradual slopes and using the Conveyor Carpet Lifts as long as you would like during operating hours.
