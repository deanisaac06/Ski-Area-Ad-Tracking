4-PACK PURCHASING DETAILS
Tickets can be used any day across the 2026+27 ski/ride season - no restrictions or blackout dates.
Canadians can purchase AT PAR.  Visit Tramside Customer Service with valid proof of current Canadian residency, such as a driver's license, and pay with Canadian cash. Check Customer Service hours HERE before making the trip. All credit card purchases are processed in USD.
4-packs can be purchased online and redeemed at Jay Peak at any lift ticket kiosk on Tramside or Stateside. Or, you can purchase on-site any day Tramside Customer Service is open across the sales period (confirm schedule HERE).
All 4 tickets are printed at the same time.
Tickets can only be printed for the original purchaser who, in turn, can share them out.
Only the original purchaser can request one-time re-prints at a cost of $10 per lift ticket.
Tax and fees are not included.
No refunds, returns, extensions, or cash value. Not for resale.
Note: Only take the tickets you want to use through the lift gates. Any tickets that are scanned will be consumed on that date. Put any extra tickets away in a safe place before heading to the lifts.
