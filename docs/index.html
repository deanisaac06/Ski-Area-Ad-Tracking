Winner score (0–100). Neither Meta nor Google shows spend or results, so the score reads what resorts reveal by their behavior — they keep paying for ads that work:
  • Run length compared with the resort's normal (60 pts). 40 days means more at a resort whose ads usually last 10 days. Ads still running only count their days so far.
  • Staying power (20 pts). Full points at 90+ days. Google uses days actually served.
  • Multiple versions (20 pts). Several versions or dynamic creative running together.
Ads under a week old show “Too early.” Meta shows US ads only while they run, so Meta history starts when tracking began (Nov 10, 2026); Google history goes back years.

Vs peers. Each ad's run length is compared with the same ad type (for example Google video) at resorts with a similar estimated ad budget, using the Ski Area Ad Atlas. “Beats 80%” means it ran longer than 80% of those ads. Small groups widen to neighboring budget classes, then to all tracked resorts. Resort rankings in the sidebar sorts resorts by estimated spend into Major, Large, Mid and Small budget classes.
Updated automatically: Meta every Monday and Thursday, Google every Monday. Images are saved copies; campaign types, offers and themes are tagged by AI (or keyword rules until the AI reaches an ad).
