With Cyber Week unfolding, resorts are using Meta to lock in early cash flow before strict November 30 deadlines. Sierra-at-Tahoe led activity by aggressively creative-testing $97 day tickets and $141 beginner lesson packages across dynamic video formats. Canadian properties are leaning heavily into deep discounts: Big White is touting up to 58% off lift tickets and 25% off lodging, while RED Mountain pushes its branded "RED Friday" discount cards. Meta traffic is getting all the focus right now while Google search sits completely untouched for new launches.

The longest-running winners prove that functional utility beats creative fluff. Aspen Snowmass dominates search by keeping hyper-practical Google ads live for ski rentals and lessons, while Lake Louise continuously scores on Meta with its explicit $160 Plus+ Card offer—promising three free days and 20% off. The move worth stealing is Lake Louise's direct value-math framing over vague mountain imagery. Next week, watch for resorts to pivot hard from these Cyber Week ticket deals into urgent holiday lodging availability before prime December inventory sells out.
