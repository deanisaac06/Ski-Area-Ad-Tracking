Mid-November signals the final rush for season pass deadlines alongside early holiday ticket pack drops. Mountain Collective pushed hard ahead of its price increase, while Epic broadcasted deadlines paired with 5 Friend Tickets and $79 Day Passes. Bromont led regional creative volume with targeted offers like $209 night and $339 weekday passes. Meanwhile, Powder Mountain pulled its $99 afternoon ticket campaign after three weeks to pivot heavily into holiday inventory, testing $149 Christmas Day lift tickets and $599 4-day Flex Passes. Regional operators like Big White and Revelstoke also launched early Black Friday multi-day card deals to capture early planners before mid-month cutoffs.

Top-performing evergreen ads rely on Google Video to build destination authority or eliminate conversion friction, highlighted by Ikon’s long-running $0 down, 0% APR financing creative. The sharpest strategy worth stealing is Powder Mountain’s explicit anti-mega-pass positioning bundled with kids-ski-free messaging, as well as Bousquet’s push on fully transferable multi-day packs. Next week, pull down expired pass countdown ads and shift mid-funnel spend directly into giftable multi-day packs and non-blackout flex cards before Black Friday CPMs surge across Meta and Google.
