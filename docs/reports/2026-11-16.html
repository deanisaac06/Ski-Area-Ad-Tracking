Resorts are still leaning heavily on video to keep top‑of‑mind as the season ramps up. Whistler Blackcomb and Park City are running pure‑brand clips that showcase destination vibe rather than a specific deal, while Jay Peak is pushing its year‑round indoor waterpark with a summer‑activities narrative. Early‑bird pass economics dominate the mix: Mountain Collective’s “buy early and save” video and static ads, Ikon Pass’s $0‑down, 0%‑APR payment plan, and Revelstoke’s lodging push that forces bookings before Nov. 15. No fresh creative batches launched this week, and nothing was pulled, indicating a hold‑steady strategy rather than a test‑and‑learn sprint.

The ads that survive longest all use high‑impact video paired with a clear, time‑bound incentive—either a pass discount or a booking deadline. Even the static and text pieces that linger (Mountain Collective’s pass graphic, Bromley’s vibe‑focused copy) echo the same urgency cue. The one outlier worth borrowing is the concise text format that still drives brand recall with minimal spend, as seen in Bromley’s “fun things never change” line.
