Mid-November brings the final pass-purchase sprint and early Black Friday launches. Enterprise passes control feed share: Ikon dropped a massive batch promoting its $800 Squad Pack ($219 off) and Deer Valley access, alongside Mountain Collective’s $759 push. Regionals are relying on firm deadlines, with Alyeska launching 40% off lodging through December 3 and Tremblant setting a November 26 cutoff for 20% off rooms. Meanwhile, Powder Mountain is carving out beginner share with a $799 lesson, rental, and pass bundle.

The industry’s longest-running, top-scoring ads prioritize utility over flash sales. Aspen Snowmass leads performance benchmarks on Google by capturing ancillary demand through ski rental ads and 20% advance lesson discounts, while Taos Ski Valley tops Meta with evergreen holiday lifestyle video. Independents should steal Powder Mountain’s strategy of bundling instruction, gear, and access into one simple price point to lower beginner barrier to entry. Next week, audit your retargeting layers to ensure hard deadline triggers are live before Black Friday ad noise peaks.
